# Kallum

## Basic Information

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Tiefling (described as purple/red at different times) |
| **Class** | Blood Hunter |
| **Original Group** | [[Neerah]], [[Ronbok]], [[Krie]] |
| **Status** | Active antagonist — last seen in Avernus |

---

## History

- Originally part of an **adventurer group** with [[Neerah]], [[Ronbok]], and [[Krie]]
- Met the party **early in the campaign** near Salsvault, fighting a Frost Giant together
- **Fell to the corruption** of one of the [[Five Rings of Tiamat]]

---

## Powers & Transformation

- Can **transform into an Ancient Red Dragon**
- During the Dumglod fight:
  - Started **chanting related to Tiamat**
  - Was grappled by the party
  - **Transformed into a dragon** and escaped

## The Attack on Rexxentrum

- **Attacked Rexxentrum** with multiple dragons — blue, red, and black
- **Destroyed the city** in the assault

---

## Scrying Vision

[[Ishanah]] scryed on Kallum through the crystal ball:
- Located in a **vast devil city** (Avernus) — iron, brass, and bone architecture
- **Part-dragon** in appearance
- **Wielding two** of the [[Five Rings of Tiamat]]
- **Hunting [[Brennan]]** for the **"final key"**

---

## Relationships
