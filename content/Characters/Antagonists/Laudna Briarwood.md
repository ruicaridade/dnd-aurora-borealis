## The Plague

Laudna Briarwood is the creator of the unnatural plague that devastated [[Korin]]'s village near [[Nicodranas]] when he was twelve. The creeping blight withered life and was incurable by conventional means. The Dwendalian Empire's response — quarantining and razing the village — killed [[Korin]]'s parents ([[Marcus Oakhart]] and [[Elara Oakhart]]) and separated him from his sister [[Faye Oakhart|Faye]].

The Empire's own investigation eventually confirmed Laudna as the plague's architect. [[Captain Eva Rostova]] uncovered this truth independently and revealed it to [[Korin]] on his eighteenth birthday, setting him on his path of vengeance.

> Although Laudna shares the infamous Briarwood name — the same family that seized Whitestone before being defeated by Vox Machina — [[Korin]] has no idea of any connection between them. Delilah Briarwood is already dead by this point in history.
