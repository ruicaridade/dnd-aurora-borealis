# Aldur

## History

- [[Dristuul]]'s brother
- **Imprisoned in the [[Obsidian Prison]]** beneath [[Shalarag]]
- Was chained to an obsidian pillar, watched by armored figures

## Early Appearances

- Appeared early in the campaign wearing **black clothes and a porcelain mask**
- **Freed the party from Dumglod prison** using his assassins
- Had **assassins** that assisted during the Dumglod escape
- Gave the **order to flee** during the [[Kallum]] dragon fight
- Knew [[Mara Veyra]] personally

---

## Imprisonment & Rescue

### Scrying Vision
When [[Dristuul]] scryed on Aldur through the crystal ball:
- Aldur was **exhausted**, chained to the obsidian pillar
- Called himself a **"nothing"**
- Said: *"The true Raven will come"*

### Death & Resurrection
- Was **dead when freed** from the prison — no pulse
- **Resurrected by [[Nyxara]]** using Revivify, with help from the whole party
- The **diamond broke** during the resurrection casting
- A **raven's feather** fell onto his chest during the resurrection — a sign from the [[Raven Queen]]

## The Black Senate

- [[Aldur]] is an **active member** of the [[Black Senate]], a secretive organization from which he gained his powers.
- He joined a couple of years ago and is **not high-ranking** — he does not yet wear a mask.
- He travels frequently for the organization.
- He **fancies a woman who wears a porcelain mask** — a high-ranking member.
- He confided all of this to [[Dristuul]] in **Draconic** during a night watch so others couldn't overhear (Session 56).
- [[Dristuul]] warned him to be careful, especially around **Robin**.
- He offered to introduce [[Dristuul]] to **Lesha**.
- He **does not want to talk about [[Mara Veyra|Mara]]** and told [[Dristuul]] not to bring her up.

---

## Relationships

- **[[Dristuul]]** — His brother. Dristuul searched tirelessly and freed him from the [[Obsidian Prison]]. Both share a connection to the [[Black Senate]]. They exchanged "I love you" during a heartfelt night watch (Session 56).
- **[[Nyxara]]** — The cleric who brought him back from death.
- **[[Mara Veyra]]** — Knew her personally; she recognized [[Dristuul]] as Aldur's brother. Aldur refuses to discuss her.
- **[[Raven Queen]]** — The raven's feather at his resurrection suggests a divine connection. Said he likes the Raven Queen based on [[Dristuul]]'s description of her.
