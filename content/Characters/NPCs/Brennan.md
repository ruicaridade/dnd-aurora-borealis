# Brennan

> *Also spelled Brenan.*

## Basic Information

| | |
|---|---|
| **True Nature** | A Fiend |
| **Father** | [[Vespin Chloras]] |
| **Status** | Active; periodically returns to Avernus to recover powers |

---

## History

- **Son of [[Vespin Chloras]]** — a fiend by nature
- Despite his heritage, he **helped [[Ishanah]] and others defeat his own father**
- Normally **hides his true fiendish form**

---

## Role in the Campaign

- **Sent a telepathic message to [[Korin]]** about finding [[Elmin Borvis]]
- **Taught the phrase** *"Gûl melin nethad"* which activates portal objects to [[Vokarth Academy]]
- **Created a teleportation circle** to transport the party to [[Vokarth Academy]] (took 2-3 days to cast)
- **Needs to return to Avernus** periodically to recover his powers

## The Breach

- Was **pulled into a breach in the ground** in Alfield
- Before disappearing, told the party to **get to Rexxentrum as soon as possible**
- [[Kallum]] is hunting him for the **"final key"**

---

## Relationships

- **[[Vespin Chloras]]** — His father. Brennan betrayed him and helped the heroes defeat him.
- **[[Ishanah]]** — An ally from the original fight against Vespin Chloras.
- **[[Korin]]** — Communicated telepathically with Korin about finding [[Elmin Borvis]].
- **[[Kallum]]** — Kallum is actively hunting Brennan for the "final key." Scrying showed Kallum in Avernus searching for him.
- **[[Ariameia]]** — Taught her the portal activation phrase.
