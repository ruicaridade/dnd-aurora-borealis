## Known History

- Corvin Thorian was an emperor whose bloodline still shapes Imperial politics
- He secretly fathered [[Kailas]] with a woman outside the sanctioned royal bloodlines
- Kailas's mother was persecuted and forced to flee because of that affair
- [[Lucian Thorian]] is identified as Corvin's son and the current emperor
