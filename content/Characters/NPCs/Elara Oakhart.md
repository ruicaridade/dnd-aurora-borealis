## Background

Elara Oakhart was a kind woman devoted to her family's farm and the local temple of Pelor in a village near [[Nicodranas]]. She and her husband [[Marcus Oakhart|Marcus]] raised two children — [[Faye Oakhart|Faye]] and [[Korin]] — in a peaceful, loving home.

When the unnatural plague (secretly created by [[Laudna Briarwood]]) swept through the village, Elara was among the first to fall ill. Despite [[Marcus Oakhart|Marcus]]'s desperate prayers and remedies, she weakened daily. When the Dwendalian Empire ordered the village destroyed, [[Marcus Oakhart|Marcus]] refused to leave her side. They died together.

---

## Legacy

Her wedding ring, alongside [[Marcus Oakhart|Marcus]]'s, now serves as [[Korin]]'s sacred spellcasting focus — a conduit for his divine power and a constant reminder of what he lost.
