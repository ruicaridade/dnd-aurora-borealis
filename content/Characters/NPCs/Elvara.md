## Description

- **Princess of [[Shalarag]]**, daughter of Emperor [[Kaelor IV Cheon]]
- **Discontent with her family**, drawn to the power of decay
- Sought to **break the seal** and unleash [[Falazure]]

---

## The Ritual

- Led a **dark ritual** using Prince [[Malrik]]'s blood to resurrect [[Falazure]]
- Drained [[Malrik]]'s blood while he lay unconscious
- Anchored her brother's soul somewhere deep below

## Battle & Death

- **Transformed into a shadowy blob** during the battle with the party
- **Died during the fight** — consumed by the very darkness she courted

---

## Relationships

- **[[Kaelor IV Cheon]]** — Her father, the Emperor. She betrayed his legacy.
- **[[Malrik]]** — Her brother, whose blood she used for the ritual.
- **[[Falazure]]** — The dragon god of decay she sought to unleash.
