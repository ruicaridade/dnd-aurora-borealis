## Background

Faye Oakhart is [[Korin]]'s older sister, two years his senior. They grew up together in a peaceful village near [[Nicodranas]] with their parents [[Marcus Oakhart|Marcus]] and [[Elara Oakhart|Elara]].

When the Dwendalian Empire razed their village to contain an unnatural plague, Faye and [[Korin]] hid inside their family home. The soldiers did not find them, but the flames did. As the house collapsed, [[Korin]] was knocked unconscious. Faye was never found.

---

## Current Status

In [[Korin]]'s memory — he was only twelve at the time — Faye died alongside their parents. Finding her has never been a driving motivation for him; he simply believes she is gone.

No specific physical details are known beyond sharing the same lineage as [[Korin]]. They were separated as children, and [[Korin]] would not recognize her today.

Whether Faye survived the razing is unknown.
