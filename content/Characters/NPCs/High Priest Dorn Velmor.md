## History

- **High Priest** at the Church in [[Shalarag]]
- Was under the **dark influence of [[Falazure]]**, which broke when the party defeated the aspect of the dragon god

---

## Role in the Campaign

- **Revealed the truth** about [[Falazure]]'s scales buried beneath [[Shalarag]]
- **Led the cleansing ritual** for Prince [[Malrik]], helping to save his soul
- **Led the party to [[Serena Voss]]** at the [[Citadel]]
- **Recognized the body** [[Korin]] carried as Emperor [[Kaelor IV Cheon]]
- **Realized [[Jaruul]] carries both [[Orcus]] and [[Primus]]** — identified the dual forces within the sorcerer

---

## Relationships

- **[[Jaruul]]** — Recognized the dangerous duality of [[Primus]] and [[Orcus]] within him. Jaruul once considered attacking Dorn in his sleep.
- **[[Malrik]]** — Led the ritual to cleanse and save the prince.
- **[[Serena Voss]]** — Directed the party to the Gate Captain.
- **[[Korin]]** — Recognized the king's body that Korin carried.
- **[[Falazure]]** — Was under the dragon god's dark influence until the party broke it.
