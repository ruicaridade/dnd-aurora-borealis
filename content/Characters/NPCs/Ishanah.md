## Role in the Campaign

- **Helped with vestige awakening rituals** alongside [[Moelwyn]]
- **Crystal ball scrying on [[Kallum]]:** Saw him in a vast devil city of iron, brass, and bone (Avernus), part-dragon, wielding two of the [[Five Rings of Tiamat]], hunting [[Brennan]] for the "final key"

## The Fall of Shalarag

- **Telepathically warned the party** during the Fall of [[Shalarag]] that the Antamian Empire was attacking and looking for someone
- Told them to **head to [[Jurin]]**
