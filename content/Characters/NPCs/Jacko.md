# Jacko

## Basic Information

| | |
|---|---|

| **Role** | Desert vehicle broker and guide |

---

## Role in the Campaign

- Recognized the party when they reached the desert beyond [[Nanaze]]
- Rented them a pink desert vehicle called the [[Sand Witch]] for their journey toward [[Drar]]
- Warned them to stop only near rocky formations because of the danger of sandworms
- Explained that after the destruction of [[Dumglod]], trade routes now ran directly between [[Nanaze]] and [[Drar]]

---

## Relationships

- **[[Sand Witch]]** - The vehicle he rented to the party for their desert crossing.
- **[[Aurora Borealis]]** - Met the party at the desert border and helped set them up for the road ahead.
