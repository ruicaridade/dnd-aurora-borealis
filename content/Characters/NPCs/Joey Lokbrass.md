## Notable Interactions

- [[Nyxara]] spotted his wagon on the road while the party traveled to [[Jinsan]]
- [[Jaruul]] used Disguise Self to impersonate Joey, then accused the real Joey of being a fake in front of Joey's two brothers — throwing them into utter confusion
- [[Jaruul]] then summoned a 10ft illusory giant from the [[Deck of Illusions]], terrifying Joey into begging the party for help
- The party "defeated" the illusion and negotiated a massive discount on their purchases
- [[Dristuul]] traded his [[Bottomless Flask]] for two Rings of Resistance
- [[Dristuul]] finally obtained the Rope of Climbing
