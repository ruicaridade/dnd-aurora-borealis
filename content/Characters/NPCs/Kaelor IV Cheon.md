## History

- **Emperor of [[Shalarag]]** for 22 years
- Son of **previous Emperor Aedric II Cheon**
- Had been **dead for weeks** when the party discovered his body
- Had **not been receiving visitors** for 3-4 months — a cover for his death or absence

---

## Discovery

- The party found his body in the [[Obsidian Prison]] / royal chambers
- **[[Korin]] carried his body out** of the prison
- [[High Priest Dorn Velmor]] recognized the body as the Emperor

---

## Relationships

- **[[Elvara]]** — His daughter, who betrayed the throne to pursue [[Falazure]]'s power.
- **[[Malrik]]** — His youngest son, who was used as a sacrifice in Elvara's ritual.
- **[[Korin]]** — The paladin who carried his body out with respect, chose the burial site, and fashioned a cross for the grave.
- **[[High Priest Dorn Velmor]]** — Recognized the Emperor's body. Spoke words over him at the burial on the hill overlooking [[Shalarag]].
