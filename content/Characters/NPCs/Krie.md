- **Transformed into a wooden mannequin** when "killed"
- Was actually a **copy/doll** — not the real Krie
- The same phenomenon occurred with [[Neerah]] and [[Ronbok]]

## Grimoire

- [[Jaruul]] **picked up his grimoire** after the copy was destroyed
- The grimoire closed itself automatically upon Krie's apparent death
