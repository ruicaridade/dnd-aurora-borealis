## Public Persona

- **Professor at [[Vokarth Academy]]**

## Secret Atrocities

- Secretly conducting **horrific experiments** beneath his office
- Had a **hidden lab** with surgical instruments and eviscerated bodies
- **Mind-controlled [[Ariameia]]** using a ring that forces the wearer into sleep
- **Tortured [[Kailas]]** in his lab (Kailas freed himself and joined the fight)

## Death

- When near death, **something took control** of his body and **transformed him into a demon**
- **Killed by [[Dristuul]]'s final blow**
- **Decapitated by [[Kailas]]** — poetic justice given the torture Kailas endured
