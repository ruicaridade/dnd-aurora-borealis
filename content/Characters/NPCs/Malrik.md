## History

- **Youngest son** of Emperor [[Kaelor IV Cheon]]
- Was **missing for months** before the party found him

---

## The Ritual

- Found being used in [[Elvara]]'s **dark ritual to resurrect [[Falazure]]**
- Had his **blood drained**; was unconscious when found
- His **soul was anchored** somewhere below the ritual site

## Rescue

- **Resurrected and saved** by the party's cleansing ritual led by [[High Priest Dorn Velmor]]
- Holy water was a key component (identified by [[Nyxara]])
- **Hidden beneath the Church** for protection after the ritual
- Later brought with the party to the mansion in [[Jurin]] to recover

---

## Relationships

- **[[Kaelor IV Cheon]]** - His father, the Emperor (deceased).
- **[[Elvara]]** - His sister, who betrayed him and used his blood for the Falazure ritual.
- **[[High Priest Dorn Velmor]]** - Led the cleansing ritual that saved his life and soul.
- **[[Nyxara]]** - Figured out that holy water was needed for the ritual.
