## Background

Marcus Oakhart was a retired Crownsguard Paladin renowned for his valor, who settled in a peaceful village near [[Nicodranas]] with his wife [[Elara Oakhart|Elara]]. He devoted himself to farming and family life while instilling a strong sense of justice and duty in his children, sharing stories of courage and righteousness.

When an unnatural plague (secretly created by [[Laudna Briarwood]]) struck the village, Marcus desperately prayed and sought remedies for [[Elara Oakhart|Elara]], who was among the first to fall ill. When the Dwendalian Empire ordered the village razed, Marcus — a Paladin to his core — refused to abandon his wife, perishing alongside her.

---

## Legacy

His legacy lives on through [[Korin]], who carries Marcus and [[Elara Oakhart|Elara]]'s wedding rings as his sacred spellcasting focus. [[Captain Eva Rostova]] often invoked Marcus's example when guiding [[Korin]] toward justice over blind revenge.
