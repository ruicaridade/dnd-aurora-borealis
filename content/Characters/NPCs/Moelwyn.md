## History

- One of the **original heroes** who defeated [[Vespin Chloras]]
- **Lost his left arm** during that legendary battle
- Was in **arcane hibernation** (asleep for two to three weeks) when the party discovered him at their mansion
- After waking, offered to help [[Nyxara]] with her Vathiriss problem once he fully recovers
- Connected to the [[Five Rings of Tiamat]]

## The Tower of Memory

The party entered Moelwyn's mind — the **Tower of Memory** — to save him:
- Fought a **twisted black dragon** inside his psyche
- Solved **riddles** throughout the tower
- **Freed his fragmented soul**, restoring him to consciousness

---

## Role in the Campaign

- **Helped awaken the party's Vestiges** alongside [[Ishanah]]
- Used [[Ishanah]]'s assistance for the awakening rituals
- Runs **Moelwyn's Marvelous Mysteries** in [[Palebank Village]]

## Relationships

- **[[Ishanah]]** — Fellow hero from the battle against [[Vespin Chloras]]. Prays beside him. They work together on vestige rituals.
- **[[Vespin Chloras]]** — The great evil Moelwyn helped defeat, at the cost of his arm.
- **[[Five Rings of Tiamat]]** — Connected to these artifacts through his history.
