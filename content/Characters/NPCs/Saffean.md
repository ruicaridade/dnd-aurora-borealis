## Background

Saffean was one of the caretakers at the orphanage where [[Theros]] and [[Neerah]] grew up. A Half-Elf, she had herself grown up despised by much of the population around her. Though not an orphan, she was an unwanted child — rejected in part by her own parents. This gave her a deep empathy for the outcasts in her care.

She was always gentle and protective of [[Theros]], shielding him from the prejudice directed at him because of his unusual race and appearance. She taught him everything — reading, writing, drawing, and all the basics needed to survive. Her shared experience of being different taught [[Theros]] not to look at his origin with bitterness.

---

## The Lute

When [[Theros]] and [[Neerah]] confided in her about [[Theros]]'s magical awakening at the tavern, Saffean was surprised at first but quickly understood what had happened. She explained that the instrument was a Lute and that there were magic users who channeled power through music — Bards.

That same afternoon, she disappeared for a few hours and returned with a Lute — less elaborate than the one [[Theros]] had played, but functional enough. She gave it to him, setting him on the path that would define his life.

---

## Farewell

When [[Theros]] decided to leave the orphanage a few months after [[Neerah]]'s departure, he shared a difficult farewell with Saffean — the closest thing to a mother he ever knew.
