## Role in the Campaign

- **Gate Captain** at the [[Citadel]] in [[Shalarag]]
- **Initially hostile** toward the party but was convinced by their evidence
- **Allowed limited access** to the [[Obsidian Prison]] (top level only)
- **Provided a map** of the prison to the party

## Death

- Died **valiantly** during the **Fall of [[Shalarag]]**
- Held her ground against **Antamian Empire soldiers** to the very end
- A soldier's death, defending the city she served
