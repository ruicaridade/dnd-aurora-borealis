# The Ferryman

## Basic Information

| | |
|---|---|
| **Nature** | Undead (actually a misplaced Construct from legend) |
| **Location** | A dock in the Underdark |
| **Destination** | [[Ixana]] |

---

## Description

- An **undead ferryman** at a dock in the Underdark
- Requires **payment of secrets and memories** for passage — not gold
- Actually a **misplaced Construct** from legend, not truly undead

---

## The Journey

- **Showed the party consequences** of the memories they gave him as payment
- Each secret or memory traded carried weight and meaning
- At the **journey's end**, he **transformed into a regular human** — freed from his construct nature
- Took the party to **[[Ixana]]**

---

## Mysteries

- What legend does the Construct originate from?
- Why was he misplaced at a dock in the Underdark?
- What happens to the memories he collects?
