## Abilities & Combat

- **Magical "Balls" Attack:** A signature offensive spell
- **Portal Activation:** Knows the phrase *"Gûl melin nethad"* to activate portal objects

---

## Background

- Studied at [[Vokarth Academy]], claims she was **abandoned there**
- Found by the party in **Salsvault** (a ruined lab), along with the cure for Frigid Woe
- Had the **Grimoire Infinitus**, which she left with [[Theros]] when she re-enrolled at the academy
- Physically resembles a younger [[Laudna Briarwood]] — elf, green eyes, red hair

## Connection to [[Laudna Briarwood]]

Ariameia looks like a younger version of Laudna. When [[Laudna Briarwood]] was killed, her dying words were:

> *"Too bad I thought the one that deserted was with this group... She'll come back one day."*

This strongly implies Laudna was referring to Ariameia as "the one that deserted."

---

## Key Moments

- **Said the magic phrase** *"Gûl melin nethad"* to activate portal objects (taught to her by [[Brennan]])
- **Mind-controlled by [[Loryn Vethas]]** using a ring that forced her into sleep
- **Rescued by the party** from Loryn's control at [[Vokarth Academy]]
- **Left the party** to re-enroll at [[Vokarth Academy]]
- **Left the Grimoire Infinitus** with [[Theros]]

---

## Relationships

- **[[Laudna Briarwood]]** — Ariameia resembles a younger version of Laudna. Laudna's dying words referenced "the one that deserted."
- **[[Theros]]** — Entrusted him with the Grimoire Infinitus.
- **[[Loryn Vethas]]** — The professor who recognized her and later mind-controlled her with a sleep ring.
- **[[Vokarth Academy]]** — Her home institution; she returned there after travelling with the party.
- **[[Brennan]]** — Taught her the portal activation phrase.
