## Abilities & Combat

- **Weapons:** Daggers, stealth, hit-and-hide tactics
- **Lockpicking:** Expert lockpicker
- **Psychic Whispers:** Telepathic communication
- **Dark Leap:** Shadowy teleportation ability
- **Shapeshift — Rat:** Can transform into a rat
- **Giant Wasp Form:** Has access to a giant wasp transformation
- **Acid Spit:** Innate dragonborn ability

## Vestige — Vermin Shroud

- Awakened via a **seppuku ritual** (Dristuul died and was resurrected during the process)
- The [[Raven Queen]] communed with him during the ritual and warned him to **cherish life**

## Personality

- Mischievous, stealthy, and reckless
- Admitted he is gay
- Loves beef jerky (keeps it in his pockets, sniffs it before sharing)
- Couldn't read a clock but eventually learned — only the "time to eat" position
- Has a **back tattoo** of a dragon and a donkey
- Feeds [[Sly]] jerky regularly

## The Black Senate

- [[Dristuul]] is a **former member** of the [[Black Senate]], a secretive organization. He left the organization at some point before the campaign.
- During a night watch, [[Aldur]] confided that he is also a member. [[Dristuul]] warned him to be careful — especially around **Robin**, whom he does not trust.
- [[Dristuul]] has connections to members named **Silk**, **Whisper**, **Lesha**, and **Robin**.
- [[Theros]] overheard their Draconic conversation about the Senate using Comprehend Languages and confronted [[Dristuul]] about it.

---

## Relationships

- **[[Aldur]]** — Brother. Dristuul searched for him relentlessly and freed him from the [[Obsidian Prison]]. Both share a connection to the [[Black Senate]]. Told him "I love you" during a night watch — Aldur said it back.
- **[[Yllira Quen]]** — Shopkeeper in [[Shalarag]]. They had a date and an overnight stay at her place.
- **[[Sly]]** — Raccoon companion. Very attached to Dristuul; gets jealous when Dristuul has "other pets."
- **[[Raven Queen]]** — Patron deity. Communes with her via rituals.
- **[[Dalaiah]]** — Cryptic tiefling seer aboard [[The Golden Minnow]]. Unresolved tension.
