# Jarul Nazide

> *Also spelled Jaruul.*
> *"The hollow one fills the void."*

## Basic Information

| | |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Jarul Nazide |
| **Class** | Sorcerer |
| **Connected To** | [[Primus]] (carries a shard) |
| **Dark Connection** | [[Orcus]] (via a stone fragment — piece of the Wand of Orcus) |

---

## Abilities & Combat

- **Sorcerous Burst:** Raw sorcerous damage
- **Thunderwave:** Concussive wave of force
- **Haste:** Accelerates an ally
- **Dispel Magic:** Removes magical effects
- **Mirror Image:** Creates illusory duplicates
- **Aid:** Bolsters allies' vitality
- **Disguise Abilities:** Can alter appearance
- **Deck of Illusions:** Uses cards to summon illusory creatures for chaos

## Items

- **Mysterious Clock:** Received from [[Dristuul]]; transported Jaruul and others to [[Mechanus]]
- **Blood Ritual Chalice:** Taken from the Falazure ritual. Capable of storing souls. Currently holds **3 souls** collected from assassins.
- **[[Krie]]'s Grimoire:** Picked up after Krie's "death"
- **Orcus Stone Fragment:** Found in the Beholder's lair; a piece of the Wand of Orcus

---

## The Dual Burden

Jaruul carries both **[[Primus]]** and **[[Orcus]]** within him, as identified by [[High Priest Dorn Velmor]]. This duality is deeply dangerous.

### Dark Vision

Jaruul had a vision of himself becoming the **destroyer of [[Mechanus]]** under the influence of [[Orcus]]:

> *"The hollow one fills the void."*

---

## Personality

- Chaotic and unpredictable
- Uses his Deck of Illusions liberally (summoned an illusory giant to scare [[Joey Lokbrass]])
- Creative problem-solver who favors dramatic solutions
- Once tried to attack [[High Priest Dorn Velmor]] in his sleep but changed his mind

---

## Key Moments

- **Jumped inside a Hydra** to cast Thunderwave from within; burrowed into its wound
- **Created statues of the party** for the banquet
- **Impersonated [[Joey Lokbrass]]** using Disguise Self and accused the real Joey of being a fake in front of his brothers, throwing them into confusion
- **Summoned illusory giant** from the [[Deck of Illusions]] to terrorize [[Joey Lokbrass]], leading to a massive discount
- **Considered attacking [[High Priest Dorn Velmor]]** in his sleep — ultimately chose not to

## Enchanted Mirror Vision

- Saw **nothing** — the mirror showed him emptiness
- His gaze specifically **breaks the mirror** each time he looks into it

## Relationships

- **[[Primus]]** — Carries a shard of the Modron god within him.
- **[[Orcus]]** — Connected via the stone fragment from the Beholder's lair. A dark and growing influence.
- **[[High Priest Dorn Velmor]]** — The priest recognized the dual forces within Jaruul.
- **[[Dristuul]]** — Gave Jaruul the mysterious clock that led to [[Mechanus]].
- **[[Krie]]** — Took Krie's grimoire after his apparent death.
