## Backstory

### Origins

Nyxara was not meant to exist. Her mother was a Kalashtar — one of the people who fight against Dal Quor's tyranny — and her father was an Inspired, a mortal vessel for the evil Quori. Two opposite forces. She was orphaned; she never met her parents and does not know what happened to them or how she came to be.

### The Tainted Bond

Like all Kalashtar, Nyxara is bound to a Quori spirit from the dream plane. But unlike her kin, who share a connection to spirits of light, hers was tainted by **Vathiriss, the Whispering Hunger of the Dreaming Dark**.

While Kalashtar do not dream, Nyxara's mind connects to the dream realm. Vathiriss manifests as nightmares, taking the shapes of her greatest fears in a constant struggle to fracture her mind. Her people feared her — a cursed Kalashtar doomed to be devoured by the nightmares. They exiled her.

### Finding the Moonweaver

Lost and wandering aimlessly, Nyxara found sanctuary in a decrepit temple of the Moonweaver. There she found the goddess's grace, who taught her to wield soothing light to keep the shadows at bay.

She sought the Moonweaver's counsel, hoping to find a way to silence the nightmares. She follows the whispers of the goddess who defied the darkness — just as Nyxara wishes to do.

### The Oath

She became a beacon for the lost, a guardian of those plagued by restless sleep and unseen horrors. Under the silver light of the moon, she swore an oath:

> *"As Moonweaver's light stands against the endless night, so shall I stand against the creeping dark. I will be the dreamer who does not wake in terror. I will be the last bastion against the void."*

### The Ongoing Struggle

Through her faith, Nyxara learned to push back against Vathiriss's influence — though the battle is never won. She continues to struggle each night, feeling its whispers: pleading, bargaining, and threatening.

She lives with the looming fear that one day Vathiriss might break free.

[[Moelwyn]] has offered to help her with the Vathiriss problem once he fully recovers.
