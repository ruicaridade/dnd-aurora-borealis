# Chalice of Souls

A blood ritual chalice with the power to store souls.

## History

- Taken by [[Jaruul]] from the [[Falazure]] ritual beneath [[Shalarag]]'s church (Session 48)
- [[Jaruul]] initially hid it in his "prison pocket"
- [[High Priest Dorn Velmor]] helped [[Jaruul]] attune to the chalice after realizing [[Jaruul]] carries the influence of both [[Orcus]] and [[Mechanus and Primus|Primus]] (Session 50)

## Properties

- Capable of storing souls
- Vibrates in the presence of soul-related phenomena

## Notable Uses

- [[Jaruul]] collected 3 souls from ninja assassins who pursued a fake Prince [[Malrik]] (Session 51)
- Vibrated during the cleansing ritual for Prince [[Malrik]], revealing a tattoo on the prince and helping return part of his soul (Session 49)
