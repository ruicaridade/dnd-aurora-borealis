# Crystal Ball

A scrying focus found in [[Moelwyn]]'s shop in [[Palebank Village]]. Affectionately named "Jeniffer" by [[Korin]].

## History

- Found behind an illusory door in [[Moelwyn]]'s shop "Moelwyn's Marvelous Mysteries" in [[Palebank Village]] (Session 33)
- [[Dristuul]] attuned to it after [[Korin]] named it "Jeniffer" (Session 35)
- Currently used by [[Ishanah]]

## Notable Scrying Sessions

- **[[Laudna Briarwood]]**: Stone temple underground, elf with green eyes, red hair, white streaks. She noticed the scrying and laughed maniacally. Confirmed she looks like an older [[Ariameia]]. (Session 35)
- **[[Neerah]]**: Dripping water, torch, obsidian pillar with chains, giant creature. Runes syphoning her energy. Creature said "YOU'RE ALMOST OUT OF TIME" in infernal. (Session 35)
- **[[Aldur]]**: Chained to an obsidian pillar, exhausted. A creature said "The Lord will be back soon." [[Aldur]] responded: "Someone will come for me!" (Session 35)
- **[[Kallum]]**: Vast devil city of iron, brass, bone. Part-dragon with two rings. Hunting [[Brennan]] for the "final key." (Session 37, scryed by [[Ishanah]])
