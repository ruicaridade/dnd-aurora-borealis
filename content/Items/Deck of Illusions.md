# Deck of Illusions

A magical deck of cards that summons illusory creatures when drawn.

## History

- Found in a chest in the Beholder's lair beneath [[Nicodranas]] (Session 26)
- The chest was behind an illusory wall and exploded with magical flames when [[Dristuul]] tried to force it open; [[Jaruul]] dispelled the magic

## Notable Uses

- [[Jaruul]] used one card to summon an illusory giant against [[Joey Lokbrass]], creating chaos that led to a huge discount on magical items (Session 30)
- 28 cards remaining after that use

## Properties

- Each card summons a different illusory creature
- Primarily wielded by [[Jaruul]] for maximum chaos
