# Dragon Mask of the Queen

The Dragon Mask of the Queen is a lost artifact tied to [[Tiamat]] and the larger struggle surrounding her return.

---

## Overview

Very little is currently known about the mask beyond its apparent connection to [[Tiamat]]'s power. After the fall of [[Shalarag]], the party speculated that it could become [[Kallum]]'s next target if he seeks another path to increasing his strength.

---

## Current Theories

- May hold a fragment or conduit of power connected to [[Tiamat]]
- May be one of the alternative paths [[Kallum]] could pursue if the [[Five Rings of Tiamat]] are not enough
- Its current location is unknown

---

## Connections

- Connected to [[Tiamat]]
- Considered a possible target for [[Kallum]]
