# Grimoire Infinitus

A [[Vestiges of Divergence|Vestige of Divergence]], twisted and transformed over the course of the campaign.

## History

- Originally belonged to [[Ariameia]]
- She entrusted it to [[Theros]] when she was re-admitted to [[Vokarth Academy]] and left the party (Session 17)
- Was briefly in [[Krie]]'s possession — it closed automatically when Krie "died"
- Twisted by [[Titania]]'s power when Hazel was merged with it during [[Theros]]'s secret visit to the Fey Wild (Session 26)

## Awakening

- Awakened by [[Theros]] in Session 37
- During the ritual, [[Theros]] blacked out and saw a possible future: a happy life with [[Neerah]] and a son, fame and recognition
- A bird warned him: "this is not your tale"
- [[Theros]] rejected the fantasy by burning the book, and the [[Grimoire Infinitus]] awakened

## Ritual Materials

- Required an enchanted mirror as a component for the awakening ritual
