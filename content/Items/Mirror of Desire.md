# Mirror of Desire

An enchanted mirror that shows the viewer their deepest desire.

## History

- Purchased from [[Joey Lokbrass]] along with other magical items (Session 30)
- Cracked when [[Jaruul]] looked into it — his gaze specifically breaks the mirror
- Repaired with Mending cast by [[Nyxara]], [[Theros]], and [[Jaruul]], with [[Dristuul]] joining in support
- After repair, looking again showed the same visions

## Visions Shown

| Character | Deepest Desire |
|-----------|---------------|
| [[Theros]] | [[Neerah]] |
| [[Dristuul]] | [[Aldur]] |
| [[Kailas]] | His brother |
| [[Jaruul]] | Nothing |
| [[Nyxara]] | A shadow creature (quori) |
| [[Korin]] | A red-haired elven woman with green eyes, resembling [[Ariameia]] / [[Laudna Briarwood]] |

## Notes

- [[Jaruul]] seeing nothing in the mirror is notable and possibly connected to his fractured identity between [[Orcus]] and [[Mechanus and Primus|Primus]]
- [[Jaruul]]'s gaze is what specifically breaks the mirror each time — suggesting something deeply unusual about his nature or void
- [[Korin]]'s vision foreshadowed the connection between [[Ariameia]] and [[Laudna Briarwood]]
