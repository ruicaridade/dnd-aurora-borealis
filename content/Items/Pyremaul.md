# Pyremaul

A massive red-iron maul with a visibly burning head. Legendary [[Vestiges of Divergence|Vestige of Divergence]].

## History

- Found in [[Moelwyn]]'s shop "Moelwyn's Marvelous Mysteries" in [[Palebank Village]], encased in a trapped glass case
- [[Kailas]] and [[Korin]] broke the glass case to retrieve it; [[Kailas]] claimed it as his weapon

## Awakening

- Awakened through a trial by fire in Session 38
- [[Kailas]] wore the ring of [[Tiamat]] and was transported to an island surrounded by lava
- He fought flaming wisps, fire giants, and a giant statue of himself
- Was transformed into a plant multiple times during the trial
- Defeated the statue and caught the awakened [[Pyremaul]]
- Afterward, [[Kailas]] couldn't remove [[Tiamat]]'s ring — [[Theros]] cast Sleep on him while [[Moelwyn]] and [[Korin]] removed it

## Notable Uses

- Used by [[Kailas]] to light the way through the mountain mine caves (Session 43)
