# Whispersting

A magical needle found in [[Moelwyn]]'s shop in [[Palebank Village]].

## History

- Discovered in [[Moelwyn]]'s shop "Moelwyn's Marvelous Mysteries" in [[Palebank Village]] (Session 33)
- Protected by a non-magical trap
- [[Dristuul]] was poisoned by purple light on his first attempt to disarm the trap, but succeeded on the second try
- Recovered and claimed by [[Dristuul]]
