# Alfield

Alfield is a small village in Wildemount. It is where [[Brennan]] was lost to a mysterious breach in the ground, and where the party purchased supplies for overland travel.

---

## Overview

A quiet, unassuming village in the Wildemount region, Alfield became the site of a dramatic event when [[Brennan]] was pulled into a breach that opened in the ground. The party also used Alfield as a resupply point, purchasing a carriage and map.
