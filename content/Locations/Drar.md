# Drar

Drar is a desert destination connected to the trade routes beyond [[Nanaze]].

---

## Overview

The party set out for Drar aboard the [[Sand Witch]] after learning that trade had shifted there in the wake of [[Dumglod]]'s destruction. [[Moelwyn]] is also said to have a contact there who might provide information.

---

## Connections

- Linked to [[Nanaze]] by desert trade routes
- Destination of the party's Sand Witch journey
- A place where [[Moelwyn]] may have useful contacts
