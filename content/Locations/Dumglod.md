# Dumglod

*Also written as Dumeglod or Doomglod.*

Dumglod is a fortified city built around a castle, visited by the party early in the campaign. Beneath its imposing walls lay a dark secret: the city's power was drawn from an imprisoned elemental.

---

## Overview

Dumglod was ruled by figures including [[Sylva Goldenroar]], whose authority led directly to the party's imprisonment after [[Dristuul]] caused offence. The city featured a castle with a prison, a magic item shop, and a network of caves beneath its foundations.

Its current political state is uncertain. After the party removed [[Nivali]], the elemental power source that sustained the city, later reports described Dumglod as falling into severe disarray, with pillaging and refugees fleeing the ruins.

---

## Key Locations

### The Castle
- Seat of Dumglod's rulers
- Contains a prison where the party was held captive
- The party inquired about the elemental [[Nivali]] while at the castle
- [[Aldur]] freed the party by breaking through a hole in the floor, leading them into the caves below

### Magic Item Shop
- The party visited but found the prices unreasonable

### Beneath the Castle
- **Caves** accessible from the prison floor
- **Giant Spider's Lair** - encountered during the escape
- **Crystal Chamber** - [[Nivali]], an elemental, was found trapped within crystals, serving as the city's energy source
- [[Nivali]] was ultimately captured in [[Theros]]'s Summerstone during a battle with [[Kallum]]'s group

---

## Events

- The party spoke with a **beggar** in the city who shared information about the **Drum La'thur war**
- [[Sylva Goldenroar]], a Rakshasa and ruler of Dumglod, undid [[Ariameia]]'s invisibility while the party was in the city
- The party was **imprisoned** by Dumglod's rulers after [[Dristuul]] caused offence
- [[Aldur]] orchestrated a prison break through the castle floor, leading the party into the underground caves
- In the caves, the party discovered that [[Nivali]] was being used as a living power source for the entire city
- With [[Nivali]] removed, Dumglod appears to have fallen into severe disorder, leaving Sylva's current rule uncertain
- Later reports described Dumglod as **ruined and ravaged by pillagers**, with refugees fleeing the city after its fall
