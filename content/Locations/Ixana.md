# Ixana

Ixana is a forested area where [[The Ferryman]] delivered the party from the Underdark. The party's mansion is located nearby, and the area is home to [[Sly]] the raccoon.

---

## Overview

A wooded region that serves as a surface exit point from the Underdark, courtesy of [[The Ferryman]]. Ixana is a relatively quiet area with a church and proximity to the party's residence.
