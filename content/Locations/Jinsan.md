# Jinsan

Jinsan is a coastal town with a harbour where the party's adventure truly began. It is where they gained their freedom and set sail aboard [[The Golden Minnow]].

---

## Overview

A port town that marks the starting point of the campaign. The party gained their freedom in Jinsan and used its harbour to embark on their journey. The town is connected to [[Palebank Village]] through its inn owner, Urgon, whose brother Erlo leads the distant village.

---

## Key Locations

### The Lazy Crew Inn
- Owned by **Urgon**, brother of Erlo (leader of [[Palebank Village]])
- A gathering place in the town

### The Harbour
- Where the party boarded [[The Golden Minnow]]
- A functional port for coastal travel

### The Colosseum (Shut Down)
- A former arena, now closed
- The previous party member **Ziltoid** was taken from here by **people in purple robes**

---

## Events

- The party **gained their freedom** in Jinsan — the true beginning of their adventure
- **Ziltoid**, a former party member, was abducted by people in purple robes from the colosseum
- The colosseum was subsequently shut down
- The party boarded [[The Golden Minnow]] at the harbour, sailing to [[Palebank Village]]

---

## Notable NPCs

- **Urgon** — owner of the Lazy Crew Inn, brother of Erlo from [[Palebank Village]]
- **Ziltoid** — former party member, taken by people in purple robes

---

## Connections

- Harbour connects to [[Palebank Village]] via [[The Golden Minnow]]
- Urgon's brother Erlo leads [[Palebank Village]]
