# Jurin

Jurin is the place where the party's mansion is located, and the destination [[Ishanah]] directed them toward after the fall of [[Shalarag]].

---

## Overview

Jurin serves as the party's road-home destination in the aftermath of the Antamian assault on [[Shalarag]]. It is associated with the party's mansion and their return to relative safety after the city's destruction.

---

## Key Locations

### The Party's Mansion
- The party's home in Jurin
- A place of refuge the group was eager to reach after fleeing [[Shalarag]]

---

## Events

- [[Ishanah]] warned the party to head to Jurin during the fall of [[Shalarag]]
- The party travelled here by wagon after escaping the city
- The group returned to the mansion here on the 5th of Unndilar

---

## Connections

- Linked to the party through their mansion
- Reached after fleeing [[Shalarag]]
