# Nanaze

*Also written as Nanaze.*

Nanaze is a city where [[Brennan]] awaited the party and revealed critical knowledge about [[The Calamity|the Calamity]] and the [[Five Rings of Tiamat]]. The party maintains a residence here and has taken on various quests from the local tavern.

---

## Overview

Nanaze serves as a base of operations for the party. [[Brennan]] used this city as a meeting point, spending 2-3 days constructing a teleportation circle here. The city has a variety of shops, a tavern with a quest board, and a church connected to a graveyard by bridges.

By Session 59, the city had changed dramatically. Refugee camps crowded the outskirts, the air carried a strange smell, many shops were closed for fear of looting, and only the wealthier districts felt even slightly secure.
