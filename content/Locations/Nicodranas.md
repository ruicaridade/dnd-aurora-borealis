# Nicodranas

Nicodranas is a coastal city where [[Nyxara]] joined the party. Beneath its surface lies a sinister cult worshipping a Beholder, and deeper still, a passage to the Underdark.

---

## Overview

A port city with a temple dedicated to the Moonweaver, Nicodranas initially appeared as a typical coastal settlement. However, the party uncovered layers of darkness beneath the city — a cult, a Beholder's lair, alchemical laboratories, and ultimately a connection to the Underdark itself.

---

## Key Locations

### Temple of the Moonweaver
- A place of worship in the city

### The Streets
- The party encountered **bullies in black and red robes** tormenting an old man
- A **bum/preacher** was found on the streets — when [[Dristuul]] used telepathy on him, something **invaded [[Dristuul]]'s mind**

### Underground Cult Hideout
- Home to a cult worshipping the **"Many-Eyed Redeemer"**
- Located beneath the city streets

### Beholder's Lair
- Found beneath the cult hideout
- Contains **deformed tentacle creatures** serving the Beholder
- The Beholder is the "Many-Eyed Redeemer" the cult worships

### Deeper Level — Alchemy Lab
- Below the Beholder's lair
- Contains **alchemy supplies** and equipment
- A **floating black stone** was found here that showed **visions of the past**

### Deepest Level — Underdark Connection
- The lowest point beneath Nicodranas
- Connected to the **Underdark** via [[The Ferryman]]

## Events

- [[Nyxara]] joined the party in Nicodranas

- [[Dristuul]] attempted telepathy on a street preacher, and something **invaded his mind**
- The party uncovered an underground cult dedicated to the "Many-Eyed Redeemer"
- Beneath the cult, a **Beholder's lair** was discovered, along with deformed tentacle creatures
- A floating black stone in the alchemy lab revealed **visions of the past**
- The party found a passage to the Underdark, accessible through [[The Ferryman]]
- The party diverted here after the destruction of [[Rexxentrum]]

---

## Nearby — Korin's Village (Destroyed)

A peaceful farming village once stood near Nicodranas, home to [[Korin]] and his family — [[Marcus Oakhart]], [[Elara Oakhart]], and [[Faye Oakhart|Faye]]. When an unnatural plague created by [[Laudna Briarwood]] struck the village, the Dwendalian Empire quarantined and razed it, killing the adults and burning every structure. [[Korin]] was the only known survivor, found unconscious in the ruins by [[Captain Eva Rostova]]. His sister [[Faye Oakhart|Faye]] was never found.
