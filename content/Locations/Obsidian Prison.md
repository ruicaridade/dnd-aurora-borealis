# Obsidian Prison

The Obsidian Prison is a terrifying underground dungeon located beneath the [[Citadel]] in [[Shalarag]]. Its lower levels are reserved for special prisoners, held in place by floating obsidian pillars bound with chains.

---

## Overview

A multi-level prison complex carved beneath [[Shalarag]]'s [[Citadel]]. The upper levels function as a standard prison, while the lower levels are something far more sinister — a cold, oppressive space where the air tastes of iron and blood, and prisoners are chained to floating obsidian pillars.

---

## Levels

### Upper Level — Regular Prison
- Functions as a standard holding facility
- Guarded by **Ned**, a prison guard
- Overseen by the **Platinum Warden**

### Lower Level — Special Prisoners
- Noticeably colder than the upper level
- The air tastes of **iron and blood**
- Features **floating obsidian pillars** bound by heavy chains
- Reserved for high-value or dangerous prisoners

---

## Prisoners

- **[[Aldur]]** — found chained and unconscious to one of the obsidian pillars, watched over by an armored figure
- **[[Neerah]]** — chained with **glowing runes** that were actively syphoning her energy

---

## Guards & Security

- **Ned** — guard on the upper level
- **Platinum Warden** — overseer of the upper prison
- **[[Varkath]]** — a black dragonborn who commands the lower level
- **Draconic guards** — serve under [[Varkath]]
- **Anti-spell wards**: The party tested for magical wards and found **none** — a bag of holding functioned normally inside the prison

---

## Events

- [[Laudna Briarwood]] appeared in the Obsidian Prison
- The party **infiltrated the prison disguised as guards and prisoners**
- Upon testing for anti-spell protections, the party discovered there were none — the bag of holding worked without issue
- [[Aldur]] and [[Neerah]] were rescued from the lower level

---

## Connections

- Located beneath the [[Citadel]] in [[Shalarag]]
- Accessed through the [[Citadel]]'s interior
