# Palebank Village

Palebank Village is a small settlement in a cold, snowy region. It holds personal significance for the party — it is where [[Dristuul]] was cured of Frigid Woe, and where the nearby ruins of [[Salsvault]] were explored. The village was later found destroyed, though one shop remained mysteriously untouched.

---

## Overview

A modest village in a frozen landscape, Palebank served as an early destination for the party. Its leader, Erlo, is the brother of Urgon from [[Jinsan]]. The village lies near [[Salsvault]], the ruined laboratory where [[Ariameia]] was discovered and the cure for Frigid Woe was found.

---

## Key Locations

### Moelwyn's Marvelous Mysteries
- A shop run by [[Moelwyn]]
- When the party returned, this was the **only building left standing** amid the village's ruins
- Protected by **Divination** and **Conjuration** enchantments
- Inside the party found:
  - **Pyremaul** — displayed in a glass case
  - Healing potions
  - A crystal ball
  - Coins and gems
  - **Whispersting** — a magical needle
- Two corpses were found inside the shop

### The Village (Ruins)
- Upon the party's return, Palebank Village was in complete ruin
- A **frozen graveyard** and **shattered homes** were all that remained
- The cause of destruction is tied to the region's dangers

---

## Events

- [[Dristuul]] was **cured of Frigid Woe** here, using the cure discovered at [[Salsvault]]
- The village itself was also cured of the spreading disease
- The party sailed here from [[Jinsan]] aboard [[The Golden Minnow]]
- On their return visit, the party found the village destroyed — only [[Moelwyn]]'s shop survived
- [[Frostmourns]] attacked the party during the return visit
