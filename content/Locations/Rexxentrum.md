# Rexxentrum

Rexxentrum was a large city in the Dwendalian Empire, now destroyed by [[Kallum]] and a flight of dragons. It was home to the [[Cobalt Soul]], which fell with the city.

---

## Overview

Once a major city of the Dwendalian Empire, Rexxentrum was obliterated in a devastating dragon attack led by [[Kallum]]. The party was en route to the city by ship from [[Zadash]] when the assault began, witnessing the destruction from the water.

---

## The Destruction of Rexxentrum

- A **blue dragon** was seen attacking the city
- A **red dragon**, ridden by [[Kallum]], joined the assault
- A third **black dragon** also appeared
- The party was aboard a ship heading to Rexxentrum when the attack commenced
- The party fought **4 Young Dragons** that attacked their ship
- After the battle, the party diverted course to [[Nicodranas]] instead of making landfall

---

## Key Locations (Pre-Destruction)

### [[Cobalt Soul]]
- A major institution of knowledge and investigation
- Located in Rexxentrum before the city's fall
