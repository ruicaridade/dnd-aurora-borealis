# Salsvault

Salsvault is a ruined laboratory near [[Palebank Village]] where [[Ariameia]] was discovered and the cure for Frigid Woe was found. The journey to reach it brought the party into contact with [[Kallum]]'s group for the first time.

---

## Overview

A crumbling research facility in the frozen wilderness near [[Palebank Village]], Salsvault once served as a laboratory — likely for alchemical or arcane research. By the time the party arrived, it had long been abandoned and fallen into ruin. Despite its state, it held two critical discoveries: [[Ariameia]] and the cure for Frigid Woe.

---

## Discoveries

### [[Ariameia]]
- Found within the ruins of Salsvault
- The circumstances of her presence in the lab remain part of her story

### Cure for Frigid Woe
- The cure was discovered in the laboratory
- Used to heal [[Dristuul]] and the people of [[Palebank Village]]

---

## Events

- The party travelled to Salsvault from [[Palebank Village]] to find a cure for Frigid Woe
- **En route**, the party encountered [[Kallum]]'s group fighting a **Frost Giant**
- Inside, the party found [[Ariameia]] and the cure
- The cure was brought back to [[Palebank Village]], saving [[Dristuul]] and the villagers
