# Sand Witch

The Sand Witch is a loud desert vehicle rented by the party for their journey from [[Nanaze]] toward [[Drar]].

---

## Overview

A fast, noisy machine built for crossing dunes, the Sand Witch requires a full crew to operate properly. [[Jacko]] rented the party a pink one and warned them to stop only near rocky formations to avoid attracting desert threats.

---

## Crew Roles

- **Driver** - [[Kailas]]
- **Navigator** - [[Korin]]
- **Mechanic** - [[Jaruul]]
- **Shooter** - [[Nyxara]]
- **Support** - [[Dristuul]]
