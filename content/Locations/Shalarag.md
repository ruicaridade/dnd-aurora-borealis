# Shalarag

Shalarag is a large, illuminated city surrounded by vineyards and farmland, ruled by the Cheon dynasty. Beneath its beauty and order lies a dark truth — the city is built over the seal binding [[Falazure]]'s essence, and the royal bloodline carries a curse. Shalarag ultimately fell to the Antamian Empire's invasion.

---

## Overview

A sprawling city with a harbour, slums, inner walls, and a citadel, Shalarag is one of the most significant locations the party has visited. Ruled by Emperor [[Kaelor IV Cheon]], with Princess [[Elvara]] and Prince [[Malrik]] as heirs, the city appeared prosperous on the surface. The party arrived via an old mountain mine from a swamp village.

## Key Locations

### [[Citadel]]
- Where the royal family resides
- Tight security throughout
- Church people can enter freely
- The [[Obsidian Prison]] lies beneath it

### [[Church of Shalarag|Church]]
- A beautiful marble building adorned with paintings of Bahamut
- Built directly over the **seal binding [[Falazure]]'s essence**
- A hidden passage behind the altar leads underground
- The scales depicted are actually those of [[Falazure]], not Bahamut

### [[Obsidian Prison]]
- Located beneath the [[Citadel]]
- Upper levels hold regular prisoners
- Lower levels hold special prisoners such as [[Aldur]] and [[Neerah]]

### Silver Tongue
- A blacksmith shop
- Capable of crafting armor from **hydra scales**

### Sewers
- Controlled by **smugglers** who move goods to and from the [[Citadel]]

---

## The Dark Truth

- The scales in the [[Church of Shalarag|Church]] are those of [[Falazure]], not Bahamut
- The royal bloodline of the Cheon dynasty is **cursed**
- The city was built over the seal containing [[Falazure]]'s essence

---

## The Fall of Shalarag

Shalarag was attacked by the **Antamian Empire**. Soldiers stormed the city, killing everyone in sight. When the party reached the throne room, the king — [[Kaelor IV Cheon]] — was found to have been **dead for weeks**. [[Serena Voss]] was killed during the assault. The party escaped south as the city fell.
