# The Golden Minnow

The Golden Minnow is an 80-foot vessel that carried the party from [[Jinsan]] to [[Palebank Village]]. Despite its captain's authority, the crew's goliath strongman is widely considered the real leader aboard.

---

## Overview

An 80-foot sailing vessel crewed by a colourful cast of characters. The Golden Minnow served as the party's first major means of travel, ferrying them from [[Jinsan]] to the frozen shores of [[Palebank Village]].
