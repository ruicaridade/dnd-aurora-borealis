# The Questionable Content

The Questionable Content is a tavern frequented by the party, known for its drinks, snacks, and a quest board that has sent them on multiple jobs.

---

## Overview

A familiar stop for the group whenever they are in town, The Questionable Content serves as both a social hub and a source of work. Even when nearly empty, it remains one of the party's go-to places for regrouping over food and drink.

---

## Notable Features

- A **quest board** the party has used to pick up local jobs
- Staff willing to indulge [[Dristuul]]'s obsession with fish jerky
- A quiet enough atmosphere for the party to trade rumors and decompress

---

## Events

- The party previously used the tavern's quest board to take on several jobs
- During Session 57, the group stopped here while [[Ishanah]], [[Neerah]], and [[Nyxara]] went to relax elsewhere
- Rudy served the party drinks and snacks while imperial soldiers marched through town outside
