# Vokarth Academy

Vokarth Academy is a prestigious institution of magical learning that exists within a **pocket dimension**, travelling across the material plane. It serves as both a school and a site of hidden secrets.

---

## Overview

Vokarth Academy is no ordinary school — it occupies a pocket dimension that drifts through the material plane, making its physical location ever-changing. Directed by [[Thalorin Dreyne]], the academy trains students in the arcane arts and hosts exhibition combat. Beneath its scholarly surface, however, lies at least one secret laboratory.

---

## Key Locations

### Grand Arena
- The academy's combat arena
- Used for **exhibition fights**
- [[Dristuul]] and [[Theros]] fought here and **lost badly**

### Library
- Staffed by **Simulacrum librarians** — magical duplicates serving as attendants
- A vast repository of arcane knowledge

### Transmission Room
- Used for magical communications

### Infirmary
- Medical facility for students and staff

### Secret Lab
- Hidden **beneath [[Loryn Vethas]]'s office**
- Its purpose and contents remain under investigation
