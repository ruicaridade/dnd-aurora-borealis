# Westholt

Westholt is the village where [[Kailas]] was born and raised before leaving on the path that led to the academy and the party.

---

## Overview

A small village within Imperial territory, Westholt is described as a settlement of roughly 1,500 people made up largely of farmers and hunters. It became a refuge for Kailas's mother after she fled the Empire.

---

## Connections

- Home village of [[Kailas]]
- The place where Kailas was raised by Garrik alongside his younger brother
- A hiding place chosen by Kailas's mother after fleeing the Empire
