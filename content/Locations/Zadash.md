# Zadash

Zadash is an enormous city within the Dwendalian Empire. The party passed through here under false identities, and the city serves as a transit point for ships heading to [[Rexxentrum]].

---

## Overview

A sprawling urban centre in the Dwendalian Empire, Zadash is notable for its size, its port connections, and its criminal underbelly. The party entered the city disguised as slavers, exploiting a surprising gap in the guards' enforcement of the Empire's own laws.

---

## Key Locations

### City Gates
- The party entered disguised as **slavers** using the aliases **Boros** and **Mariel Velk**
- Despite slavery being **illegal** in the Dwendalian Empire, the guards cooperated and let them pass

### Leakytap Tavern
- A tavern the party visited

### Criminal HQ
- A **criminal group** operates from a tavern in Zadash
- Serves as their headquarters

### Port
- Ships depart from Zadash to [[Rexxentrum]]
