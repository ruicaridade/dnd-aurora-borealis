# Black Senate

A secretive and powerful organization with connections to both [[Dristuul]] and [[Aldur]].

---

## Overview

The Black Senate is a clandestine organization whose full purpose and structure remain largely unknown. Members gain power through their affiliation. High-ranking members are distinguished by wearing **masks** — a detail [[Dristuul]] is aware of.

## Details

- [[Aldur]] confided his membership to [[Dristuul]] in **Draconic** during a night watch, so others wouldn't overhear (Session 56)
- [[Dristuul]] revealed he had also been a member but **left** the Senate. He warned [[Aldur]] to be careful.
- [[Theros]] later overheard their Draconic conversation about the Black Senate using **Comprehend Languages** and confronted [[Dristuul]] about it.
- Only **high-ranking members wear masks** — [[Aldur]] is not high enough to wear one.
