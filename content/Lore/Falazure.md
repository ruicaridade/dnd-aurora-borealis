# Falazure

**Falazure**, also known as the **Dragon God of Decay** and the **Dragon God of Death**, is a draconic deity whose influence has been deliberately hidden from mortal knowledge. His secret role during [[The Calamity]] and his connection to [[Shalarag]] form one of the campaign's most significant revelations.

---

## Role in the Calamity

During [[The Calamity]], when [[Bahamut]] and [[Pelor]] struck down [[Tiamat]], Falazure was not an idle observer. He **secretly assisted [[Tiamat]]** in her war against [[Bahamut]], providing aid that went unrecorded by historians and theologians alike.

This hidden alliance means that the accepted history of the Calamity is incomplete. [[Tiamat]]'s strength during the final battle was bolstered by Falazure's intervention, and his influence left traces that would not be understood for centuries.

---

## The Truth Beneath Shalarag

The city of **[[Shalarag]]** was built upon what its people believed to be a sacred site blessed by [[Bahamut]]. Beneath the city lie enormous **draconic scales** — long venerated as relics of the Platinum Dragon.

**They are not Bahamut's scales. They are Falazure's.**

This truth reframes the entire history and identity of [[Shalarag]]:

- The **royal family of [[Shalarag]]** is not blessed by [[Bahamut]] — they are **cursed by Falazure**. Their bloodline carries the taint of the Dragon God of Decay, not the grace of the Platinum Dragon.
- The **church in [[Shalarag]]** was built directly over a **seal** binding Falazure's essence. What was thought to be a holy site is, in reality, a prison.

---

## The Seal and Princess Elvara

Princess **[[Elvara]]** of [[Shalarag]] discovered — or was led to discover — the truth about the seal beneath the church. She **attempted to break the seal** and unleash Falazure's essence, whether out of ambition, corruption, or manipulation.

The party intervened and fought an **aspect of Falazure** beneath the church — a manifestation of the god's bound power. The battle confirmed the terrible truth about [[Shalarag]]'s foundations and the nature of the royal bloodline.

---

## Current Status

Falazure's full essence remains sealed, but the integrity of that seal is uncertain. With [[Elvara]]'s attempt and the party's battle against his aspect, the situation beneath [[Shalarag]] is precarious. The Dragon God of Decay is patient, and his influence continues to seep through the cracks.
