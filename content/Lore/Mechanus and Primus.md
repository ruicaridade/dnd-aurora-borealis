# Mechanus and Primus

## Mechanus

**Mechanus** is the plane of absolute law and order — a **Lawful Neutral** outer plane composed of enormous interlocking gears and clockwork structures. Every action within Mechanus follows precise, immutable rules. It is a place devoid of chaos, where logic and structure govern all things.

---

## Primus

**Primus** is not a name but a **title**, given to whoever leads Mechanus. The holder of the title is not necessarily immortal and has **no fixed gender** — Primus is simply the supreme authority of the plane, the embodiment of order itself.

---

## The Fall of Primus

Primus was **defeated by [[Orcus]]**, the Demon Prince of Undeath. The assault shattered Primus's essence, fragmenting it into shards that scattered across the planes.

In a final act of preservation, Primus **sent these shards outward** to find capable bodies — individuals who might serve as vessels to restore order to Mechanus and eventually reconstitute the title's power.

---

## Jaruul's Connection

**[[Jaruul]]** carries one of these shards within him, granting him a direct connection to the remnants of Primus's will. This bond ties [[Jaruul]] to the fate of Mechanus itself.

However, this connection is not without peril. [[Jaruul]] experienced a **dark vision** in which he saw himself becoming the **destroyer of Mechanus**, twisted under [[Orcus]]'s influence. The vision suggests that the same shard meant to restore order could, if corrupted, be turned into a weapon against the very plane it was meant to save.

The tension between Primus's purpose and [[Orcus]]'s designs places [[Jaruul]] at the centre of a conflict that stretches far beyond the material plane.
