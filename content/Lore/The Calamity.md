# The Calamity

The Calamity was a cataclysmic conflict among the gods that reshaped the world. Among its many battles, the war against [[Tiamat]] stands as one of the most consequential — its aftermath still ripples through the present day.

---

## The Fall of Tiamat

During the Calamity, [[Tiamat]] was defeated through the combined might of [[Bahamut]], the Platinum Dragon, and [[Pelor]] the Dawnfather. Rather than being destroyed outright, **Tiamat's soul was split in two**. One fragment was cast into **Avernus**, the first layer of the Nine Hells, where it would later be forged into [[The Five Rings of Tiamat|five powerful rings]] and scattered across continents as a safeguard.

### Distribution of the Rings

The five rings were deliberately separated to prevent any single individual or faction from reuniting them:

- **3 rings** were hidden across [[Tal'Dorei]]
- **1 ring** was entrusted to the [[Cobalt Soul]]
- **1 ring** was secured within [[Vokarth Academy]]

---

## Falazure's Secret Role

Unknown to most scholars and historians, [[Tiamat]] did not fight alone. **[[Falazure]]**, the draconic god of death and decay, secretly assisted her during the battle against [[Bahamut]]. This alliance has been buried beneath layers of myth and religious doctrine.

Most critically, the **scales discovered beneath [[Shalarag]]** — long believed to belong to [[Bahamut]] — are in truth the scales of [[Falazure]]. This revelation reframes the history of [[Shalarag]]'s royal family and the true nature of the seal beneath their church.

---

## Legacy

The Calamity's consequences continue to shape the world:

- The [[The Five Rings of Tiamat|Five Rings]] remain objects of immense power and danger
- [[Vespin Chloras]] later attempted to collect all five rings to resurrect [[Tiamat]]
- [[Falazure]]'s hidden involvement means that key historical "truths" — especially in [[Shalarag]] — are built on lies
