# The Five Rings of Tiamat

The Five Rings of Tiamat are artifacts of immense and terrible power, each forged from a fragment of [[Tiamat]]'s soul after her defeat during [[The Calamity]].

---

## Origin

When [[Tiamat]] was struck down by [[Bahamut]] and [[Pelor]], her soul was severed in two. One piece was cast into **Avernus**, the first layer of the Nine Hells. There, that fragment was used to create **five rings**, each containing a portion of the Dragon Queen's essence. The rings were then spread across different continents to prevent their reunion.

---

## Properties

Each ring grants its wearer the ability to **transform into a dragon**, channelling [[Tiamat]]'s own power. However, this comes at an extraordinary cost: **the wearer's soul is gradually consumed** by the ring. Prolonged use leads to corruption, madness, and eventual domination by Tiamat's will.

---

## The War for the Rings

### Vespin Chloras's Campaign

[[Vespin Chloras]], a powerful mage from the post-Calamity era, was manipulated by [[Tiamat]] into attempting to gather all five rings and use them to resurrect her. He was ultimately defeated by a coalition of three heroes:

- [[Moelwyn]]
- [[Ishanah]]
- [[Kallum]]

### Aftermath

After [[Vespin Chloras]]'s defeat, the three victors each took possession of one ring:

| Bearer | Outcome |
|---|---|
| [[Kallum]] | Fell to the ring's corruption and became an antagonist |
| [[Moelwyn]] | Suffered grievous injury — lost his left arm — and entered arcane hibernation to survive |
| [[Ishanah]] | Retained her ring and remained an ally |

The remaining two rings stayed in the custody of the [[Cobalt Soul]] and [[Vokarth Academy]].

---

## Current Relevance

The party encountered the rings' power firsthand when one was used during [[Kailas]]'s vestige awakening ritual. The rings remain central to the overarching conflict: so long as they exist, [[Tiamat]]'s resurrection remains possible — and those who wield them risk losing themselves entirely.
