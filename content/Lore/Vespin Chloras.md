# Vespin Chloras

**Vespin Chloras** was a powerful mage who lived in the post-[[The Calamity|Calamity]] era. Driven by ambition and deceived by [[Tiamat]]'s whispers, he became the primary agent in a failed attempt to resurrect the Dragon Queen.

---

## Background

Vespin Chloras was a formidable arcanist whose talents drew the attention of [[Tiamat]] herself. From her prison, [[Tiamat]] reached out to him, promising him unimaginable power in exchange for one task: **reunite [[The Five Rings of Tiamat|the five rings]]** forged from her soul and use them to bring her back.

Seduced by the offer, Vespin Chloras set about collecting the rings from their scattered hiding places across the continents.

---

## Defeat

Vespin Chloras was ultimately stopped by three heroes who banded together to oppose him:

- [[Moelwyn]]
- [[Ishanah]]
- [[Kallum]]

The battle was costly. [[Moelwyn]] lost his left arm and was forced into arcane hibernation. [[Kallum]], having claimed one of the rings, eventually succumbed to its corruption and turned against his former allies. Only [[Ishanah]] emerged relatively intact.

---

## Legacy

### Brennan

Vespin Chloras left behind a son: **[[Brennan]]**. [[Brennan]] is a **Fiend** — his nature tied to his father's pact with [[Tiamat]] and the infernal origins of the rings. Despite his parentage, [[Brennan]]'s loyalties are complicated, and he has aided the party on multiple occasions, including teleporting them to [[Vokarth Academy]] via a circle of teleportation.

### The Rings

Three of [[The Five Rings of Tiamat]] ended up in the hands of the heroes who defeated him. The remaining two stayed with the [[Cobalt Soul]] and [[Vokarth Academy]]. The threat Vespin Chloras sought to unleash remains very much alive.
