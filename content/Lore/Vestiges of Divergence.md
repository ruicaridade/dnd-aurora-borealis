# Vestiges of Divergence

Vestiges of Divergence are legendary magical items of extraordinary power. Unlike ordinary enchanted equipment, vestiges are **living artifacts** — they grow and change alongside their wielders, capable of being "awakened" through specific rituals.

---

## Awakening Ritual

To awaken a vestige, the following conditions must be met:

1. **A place of calm and magic** — The ritual must be performed in a location suffused with arcane energy and relative peace.
2. **A catalyst** — An object or substance connected to the deity or original wielder associated with the vestige.
3. **An act of will or sacrifice** — The wielder must demonstrate resolve, often through a personal cost.
4. **Magic cost** — The ritual requires significant magical energy, typically channelled by experienced casters. [[Ishanah]] and [[Moelwyn|Moe]] have served as the party's ritual casters.
5. **A trial or test** — The wielder undergoes a vision, faces a guardian, or is otherwise tested during the awakening.

Each vestige is **unique**, shaped by the wielder's experiences and choices. The same vestige in different hands could manifest in entirely different ways.

## Party Vestiges

### [[Korin]] — Sword of Trials
- **Status:** Awakened
- The Sword of Trials was already awakened through a prior ritual. It was further **changed by [[Korin]]'s will**, reflecting his personal growth and resolve.

### [[Dristuul]] — Vermin Shroud
- **Status:** In possession
- A vestige tied to [[Dristuul]]. Details of its awakening and full capabilities are still unfolding.

### [[Theros]] — Grimoire Infinitus
- **Status:** In possession (twisted)
- Originally belonging to [[Ariameia]], the Grimoire Infinitus was **twisted by [[Titania]]'s power** and passed to [[Theros]]. Its current form reflects that corruption, and its behaviour may be unpredictable.

### [[Kailas]] — Pyremaul
- **Status:** In possession
- A **legendary red-iron maul** discovered in [[Moelwyn]]'s shop in Palebank. [[Kailas]]'s vestige awakening ritual involved the use of one of [[The Five Rings of Tiamat]], underscoring the gravity and danger of the process.
