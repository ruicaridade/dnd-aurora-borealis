# Vokarth Academy

**Vokarth Academy** is a prestigious magical academy that exists within a **pocket dimension**. It does not have a fixed location — instead, it **travels through the material plane**, changing its point of entry periodically. This makes it both difficult to find and nearly impossible to siege.

## Notable Features

### The Forbidden Library
Vokarth Academy houses a **forbidden library section** containing dangerous and restricted texts. Access is heavily controlled, though the party has had reason to seek its contents.

### Ring of Tiamat
One of [[The Five Rings of Tiamat]] was kept within Vokarth Academy as part of the post-[[The Calamity|Calamity]] effort to keep the rings separated. The academy's pocket-dimension nature made it an ideal vault.

---

## Connection to the Party

### Ariameia
[[Ariameia]] studied at Vokarth Academy before the events of the campaign. She was "**abandoned**" by the institution under unclear circumstances, leaving a lasting mark on her. She was later **re-admitted**, though the relationship between her and the academy remains complicated.

### Arrival via Brennan
The party was **teleported to Vokarth Academy** through [[Brennan]]'s circle of teleportation, bypassing the academy's usual means of access.

### Loryn Vethas
[[Loryn Vethas]], a former professor at the academy, turned against its principles and became a villain the party was forced to confront. The party ultimately killed her during the conflict.
