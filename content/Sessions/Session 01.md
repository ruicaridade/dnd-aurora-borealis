# Session 1 - Arrival in Dumglod

The adventuring party known as [[Aurora Borealis]] found themselves in the city of [[Dumglod]], beginning what would become a tangled web of intrigue.

## Summary

- [[Aurora Borealis]] arrived in [[Dumglod]] and began exploring the city
- Visited a magic item shop but were immediately put off by the outrageous prices
- Spoke with a beggar who shared information about the [[Drum La'thur]] war
- Visited [[Dumglod]] castle seeking information about the elemental [[Nivali]]
- [[Dristuul]] managed to "insult" the rulers — a diplomatic masterclass as always
- [[Ariameia]]'s invisibility spell was dispelled by [[Sylva Goldenroar]], who turned out to be a Rakshasa
