# Session 2 - The Road to Salsvault

A retroactive summary of the party's earlier journey toward [[Salsvault]], before the events in [[Dumglod]].

## Summary

- The party was on the way to [[Salsvault]], the ruined lab where [[Ariameia]] was originally found, and where the cure for the Frigid Woe that afflicted [[Dristuul]] and [[Palebank Village]] was located
- Encountered a group locked in combat with a Frost Giant. The party wisely decided not to join that particular fight.
- The group consisted of [[Kallum]], [[Ronbok]], [[Krie]], and [[Neerah]] (an acquaintance of [[Theros]])
- After brief introductions, both groups decided to camp together for the night
- [[Dristuul]] stole the Bottomless Flask from [[Ronbok]] — because of course he did
- The groups parted ways the following morning
- Hours later, the party found a recently destroyed camp with several bodies — two of them looked disturbingly "similar" to [[Ronbok]] and [[Krie]]
