# Session 3 - Prison Break

The party found themselves behind bars in [[Dumglod]], but a mysterious ally had other plans.

## Summary

- The party was arrested and dragged to [[Dumglod]] castle prison by order of [[Sylva Goldenroar]]
- [[Dristuul]] got bored in solitary confinement (let's just say there was some crude humor involving his tail)
- [[Aldur]] appeared wearing strange black robes and a porcelain mask. He freed the party through a hole in the floor of one of the cells.
- The party entered a cold, dark, damp cave beneath the castle
- Crossed a precipice — [[Dristuul]] heroically saved [[Ariameia]] using newly discovered abilities
- Passed through a giant spider's lair without alerting it (a miracle)
- Through a secret passage opened by [[Aldur]], the party reached [[Nivali]]'s cage — the elemental was being used as [[Dumglod]]'s energy source
- Using Psychic Whispers, [[Dristuul]] communicated with [[Nivali]]. The elemental was decidedly not friendly.
- [[Theros]] tried to free [[Nivali]] with his artifact, but nothing happened
- A group of adventurers the party had met before appeared:
  - [[Kallum]] — male purple tiefling, blood hunter
  - [[Neerah]] — female elf, warlock
  - [[Ronbok]] — male dwarf, cleric
  - [[Krie]] — male blue dragonborn, wizard
