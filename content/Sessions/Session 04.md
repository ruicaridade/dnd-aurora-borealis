# Session 4 - The Crystal Chamber

Tensions exploded beneath [[Dumglod]] as [[Kallum]]'s group revealed their true intentions.

## Summary

- [[Nivali]] remained imprisoned as the elemental energy source
- [[Kallum]]'s group was present: [[Kallum]] (tiefling), [[Ronbok]] (dwarf), [[Krie]] (dragonborn), and [[Neerah]] (elf, [[Theros]]'s acquaintance)
- The new group wanted to destroy the crystals — combat began immediately
- [[Ronbok]] and [[Neerah]] transformed into wooden mannequins upon "dying" — clearly not what they seemed
- Two guards and a Minotaur entered the room and joined the fight, escalating the chaos
- The portal crystals shattered and the remaining crystals stopped emitting light
- The entire area changed — everything was engulfed in total darkness
