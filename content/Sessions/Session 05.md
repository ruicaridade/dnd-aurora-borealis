# Session 5 - The Dragon Awakens

What began as a chaotic underground battle escalated into a desperate escape from an Ancient Red Dragon.

## Summary

- [[Jaruul]] did a backflip over the Minotaur's charge — style points were awarded
- [[Ariameia]]'s magic orbs accidentally hit [[Korin]] (friendly fire is never truly friendly)
- [[Dristuul]] threw a knife at [[Krie]], who finally "died" and turned into a wooden mannequin. He dropped his grimoire, which closed automatically. [[Jaruul]] picked up the book.
- [[Theros]] began channeling the Summerstone on the Elemental. The room heated up as if a sun had appeared in its center.
- [[Kallum]] started chanting something related to [[Tiamat]] — then was grappled by [[Korin]]
- [[Kallum]] transformed into an Ancient Red Dragon
- [[Theros]] finished channeling and captured [[Nivali]] inside the Summerstone
- Assassins known to [[Aldur]] joined the fight, mentioning a spider they had encountered on their path
- [[Aldur]] ordered the party to flee. Going back through the secret passage, they found a Matriarch Spider completely eviscerated.
- Following a path behind the spider, they found an exit behind the castle and escaped
