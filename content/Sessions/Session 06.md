# Session 6 - The Search for Elmin Borvis

A telepathic message set the party on a new path, leading them to a grim discovery.

## Summary

- [[Korin]] received a telepathic message from [[Brennan]] asking them to find [[Elmin Borvis]] (this type of communication was being blocked by something)
- [[Brennan]] revealed that saying "Gul melin nethad" triggers a reaction in an object used to open portals to [[Vokarth Academy]], where [[Ariameia]] once studied
- The party obtained information about the Grimoire (the book [[Krie]]'s group had been using) through [[Theros]]'s Detect Magic
- The heroes followed [[Dristuul]]'s flawless navigation to [[Elmin Borvis|Elmin]]'s house — miraculously, no one got lost
- [[Korin]] disappeared the moment he entered [[Elmin Borvis|Elmin]]'s house after getting no response at the door
- Everyone entered to find [[Elmin Borvis|Elmin]] dead, with cuts across his chest, and the house completely ransacked
- [[Ariameia]] said the magic phrase perfectly, and several objects in the room began to glow and float toward the center
