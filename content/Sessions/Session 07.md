# Session 7 - Elmin's Trap and the Journey Begins

The party narrowly escaped a deadly trap in [[Elmin Borvis|Elmin]]'s house and received an urgent summons.

## Summary

- In [[Elmin Borvis|Elmin]]'s room, the heroes saw floating objects after [[Ariameia]] said the magic phrase
- She picked up a small box (which seemed to have held a ring) and a diary
- [[Dristuul]], not knowing what clock hands were, found a pocket watch with a secret compartment containing a photo of [[Elmin Borvis|Elmin]] and two other people
- Each party member touched the floating objects, which then fell — causing injuries and exhaustion. The room seemed to shrink around them.
- At the last moment, only the object they were looking for remained — an envelope. They barely escaped in time.
- At the tavern, the group gathered in [[Ariameia]]'s room to read the diary
- When most fell asleep from exhaustion, [[Korin]], one of the last standing, received an urgent message to head immediately to [[Nanaze]]
- Next morning: [[Dristuul]] finally learned to read the clock — but only the position indicating meal time. Priorities.
- The heroes needed transport to [[Nanaze]]. They cleverly scammed a vendor, getting money, an elephant (Eli), and elephant food to cross the desert.
