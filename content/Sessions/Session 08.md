# Session 8 - The Desert Crossing

A harrowing journey across the desert brought the party face-to-face with enormous sandworms.

## Summary

- The party journeyed across a vast desert riding a majestic elephant named Eli
- Eli had tarps strapped to his legs for protection against the scorching sand
- After a full day of travel, they rested among rocks for the night
- The next day, Eli became frightened and bolted. [[Jaruul]] grabbed a rope tied to Eli's leg and struggled to climb back up.
- The panicked elephant ran into an area full of holes in the sand
- The party was thrown from Eli's back as a massive sandworm emerged from the ground
- A fierce battle erupted against both the sandworm and its even larger matriarch
- The party killed both worms
- Eli survived the ordeal, and the party continued their desert crossing
