# Session 9 - The Calamity Revealed

The party arrived in [[Nanaze]] and learned the true scope of the threat they face.

## Summary

- Continued the desert journey after the Sandworm battle
- During camp, the party learned that [[Ariameia]] has a connection to [[Vokarth Academy]], from which she was — according to her — abandoned
- Arrived at [[Nanaze]] and met [[Brennan]], who revealed the history of [[The Calamity]]:
  - During [[The Calamity]], [[Tiamat]] was defeated by [[Bahamut]] and [[Pelor]], and her soul was split in two
  - One piece ended up in Avernus, where it was used to create the [[Five Rings of Tiamat|5 powerful rings]]
  - The rings spread across continents: 3 in Tal'Dorei, 1 in the [[Cobalt Soul]], and 1 in [[Vokarth Academy]]
- [[Vespin Chloras]], a powerful post-Calamity mage, tried to reunite the rings to resurrect [[Tiamat]], manipulated by her promises of power
- [[Vespin Chloras|Vespin]] was defeated by [[Moelwyn]], [[Ishanah]], and [[Kallum]], who kept three of the rings
- [[Kallum]] eventually fell to the ring's corruption and now plans to attack [[Vokarth Academy]] to seize another ring
- The academy exists in a pocket dimension — a circle of teleportation is needed to reach it
- The party returned to their residence for [[Brennan]] to spend 2-3 days casting the teleportation spell
