# Session 10 - Bear Hunt and Side Quests

With [[Brennan]] busy preparing the teleportation circle, the heroes took on local jobs in [[Nanaze]].

## Summary

- The heroes woke early and headed to the village, leaving [[Ariameia]] and [[Brennan]] at the house
- At the "Sam Smith & Britney Spears" shop (yes, really), they asked about crafting weapons and armor from kitin plates — far too expensive. A bribery attempt also failed.
- Shopping at "Scrolls R Us" — [[Theros]] traded a Wand of Prestidigitation for 3 Potions of Greater Health
- At "The Questionable Content" tavern, found a job posting board. [[Theros]] grabbed three quests:
  1. Kill an infected bear
  2. Find and kill terrorizing goblins
  3. Investigate the cemetery at night
- Headed to where the bear was spotted
- Purple fog appeared, and trees and flowers glowed with purple dust that caused poison damage on contact
- Found the bear resting — patches of fur missing, purple veins bulging across its body
- [[Dristuul]] climbed a tree while [[Korin]] approached. The bear awoke enraged.
- The fog was denser near the bear, affecting both [[Korin]] and [[Dristuul]]
- [[Korin]] was critically wounded by bites
- [[Theros]] used Dissonant Whispers to push the bear away and Healing Word on [[Korin]]
- The bear kept charging back — this painful cycle repeated for 3 rounds
- The party eventually defeated the beast
