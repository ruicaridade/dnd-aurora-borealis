# Session 11 - Goblins at the Bridge

Fresh off their bear victory, the party tracked down the goblin menace and delivered swift justice.

## Summary

- After killing the infected bear, the party rested before continuing to the goblin quest
- Found a clearing followed by a cliff created by waterfall erosion
- A rope and wood bridge connected both sides of the ravine
- [[Dristuul]] crossed cautiously with [[Jaruul]]'s help, ready to fly if the bridge collapsed
- [[Korin]] followed, while [[Jaruul]] and [[Theros]] stayed behind a tree on the near side
- On the other side, [[Dristuul]] saw something indescribable
- While [[Jaruul]] was crossing, [[Theros]] noticed something strange — 3 Goblins appeared on each side of the bridge, plus an old Goblin leader near [[Dristuul]]
- Quick and easy battle. [[Jaruul]] nearly destroyed the bridge with Meteorite — solving the goblin problem and almost the "getting back" problem simultaneously.
- One goblin (affectionately dubbed "Gnoblin D") ran when seeing his companions decimated
- [[Korin]] caught him with a grapple
- Interrogation attempt: [[Dristuul]] played "good cop" while [[Theros]], fed up with the lack of answers, eliminated the goblin with Eldritch Blast. So much for diplomacy.
- Returned to the village to rest before the cemetery investigation
