# Session 12 - Night at the Cemetery

The final side quest brought the party to a haunted graveyard, where the undead were not resting peacefully.

## Summary

- Short rest at the tavern before heading out for the night
- Headed toward the church. Had to cross bridges to reach the graveyard. Suspicious.
- [[Theros]] cast Invisibility on [[Korin]], who scouted ahead with Divine Sense — detected multiple Undead
- [[Jaruul]] "disguised" himself as an Undead and crossed the bridges, continuing forward with questionable confidence
- [[Jaruul]] gestured at a window, then made a crude gesture at it
- [[Jaruul]] fought a 1v1 with the window and won. Made enormous noise in the process, waking all the spirits. Torches around the area lit up.
- Combat ensued against the restless dead
- [[Korin]] used Lightning powers to annihilate numerous Ghosts and Spectres with [[Jaruul]]'s help
- [[Theros]] completely destroyed a Ghost and several Spectres
- [[Dristuul]] won his personal PvP fight against Spectre I — bragging rights secured
- [[Dristuul]] entered the church to steal holy water, which was returned minutes later by Hazel, [[Theros]]'s familiar (the familiar has more moral fiber than the rogue)
- Returned home to find [[Brennan]] destroying his bedroom door. Found this perfectly normal. Went to sleep.
