# Session 13 - Chores and Errands

A lighter session of odd jobs, petty theft, and one very bamboozled old lady.

- Checked on [[Brennan]] — his room was full of chalk and a mess (the man was working hard on the teleportation circle)

- Old lady's quest: a raccoon stole her ring. Found the animal in a tree and traded it for a gold coin.
- Got the raccoon ([[Sly]]) and the ring. Lied to the old lady about the ring's fate — [[Dristuul]] now wears it. Justice.
- Started traveling to deliver the package to another village
- Found a patch of giant mushrooms but wisely chose to ignore it
- Ambushed by bandits near a fallen tree — all bandits were killed
- Reached the destination village, delivered the package, and returned
- Gave the "alchemist" at the bar his book back
- Returned to the house — [[Brennan]] has finished the teleportation circle
- The party went to sleep, ready for what comes next
