# Session 14 - Welcome to Vokarth

The party finally reached [[Vokarth Academy]], but a grand dinner took a dark turn.

## Summary

- [[Ariameia]] appeared to not have slept for days
- [[Sly]] stayed at the mansion (raccoons are not welcome at magical academies, apparently)
- Entered [[Brennan]]'s teleportation circle; [[Ariameia]] recited the magic phrase while holding the portrait
- Greeted by hostile guards — the visit was unauthorized
- [[Loryn Vethas]] calmed the guards and welcomed them, recognizing [[Ariameia]]
- [[Vokarth Academy]] exists in a pocket dimension that travels through the material plane
- [[Thalorin Dreyne]], the academy director, received them in his office
- Invited to the start-of-year gala dinner — accepted enthusiastically for the promise of a grand feast
- After dinner, a dance. The group split up. [[Ariameia]] was seen following Professor [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]]. [[Theros]] and [[Jaruul]] followed stealthily.
- [[Theros]] overheard part of [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]] and [[Ariameia]]'s conversation — [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]] seemed to be planning something sinister
- [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]] did something to [[Ariameia]] that left her body limp
- [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]] used Dimension Door to teleport away
- [[Theros]] alerted the director. [[Brennan]] and [[Thalorin Dreyne|Thalorin]] rushed to [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]]'s office.
- A hidden mechanism under [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]]'s desk revealed a stone staircase
- Found a room with surgical instruments, eviscerated bodies, and [[Ariameia]] unconscious while [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]] conjured a spell
- [[Kailas|Kailas Fyrn]], a goliath who had been tortured by [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]], broke free from his restraints and joined the fight
