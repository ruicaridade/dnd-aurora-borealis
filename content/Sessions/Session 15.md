# Session 15 - The Demon Within

The battle against [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]] reached its terrifying climax as his true nature was revealed.

## Summary

- The fight against [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]] began in earnest. [[Kailas|Kailas Fyrn]] the Goliath's strategy was simple and effective: just keep hitting [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]].
- [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]] appeared to be draining [[Ariameia]]. After separating them, it was clear she had been placed on runes designed to siphon her energy.
- During the fight, [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]] kept desperately trying to get close to [[Ariameia]]
- When near defeat, something took control of [[Loryn Vethas|Loryn]]'s body — he transformed into a large demon-looking creature
- After a close encounter with death, [[Dristuul]] dealt the final blow
- [[Thalorin Dreyne|The academy president]] arrived and discovered [[Ariameia]] was wearing a ring that forced sleep and mind-control. She had no memory of the last several days.
- [[Kailas|Kailas Fyrn]] decapitated the demon for good measure
- Academy guards rushed in and surrounded the lab
