# Session 16 - The Plane of Mechanus

- Started the session at [[Vokarth Academy]]
- [[Jaruul]] mentioned having a strange dream but refused to give any details
- The director [[Thalorin Dreyne]] distributed rewards to the party for their efforts defending the academy
- [[Jaruul]] heard the sound of many clocks ticking from a distant, otherworldly place
- He asked to examine [[Dristuul]]'s pocket watch. While holding it, [[Korin]] noticed an unhealthy fixation in [[Jaruul]]'s eyes and tried to take the watch back — both of them vanished instantly, transported to another dimension
- The rest of the party touched the watch and were also transported
- Battled five constructs in the plane of [[Mechanus and Primus|Mechanus]]
- Met [[Mechanus and Primus|Primus]], the One and Supreme, leader of [[Mechanus and Primus|Mechanus]]
- [[Mechanus and Primus|Primus]] told them he had been defeated by [[Orcus]], and his essence was fragmented as a result
- He had sent shards of himself out to find capable bodies that could help restore order in [[Mechanus and Primus|Mechanus]]
- One shard could fix the flaw left by his defeat and take his place
- [[Mechanus and Primus|Primus]] disappeared; the clocks spun rapidly and the party was teleported back to [[Vokarth Academy]]
