- The party ate together before the big event

- Found two books on the Professor; rested and planned their strategy
- The event was much bigger than expected — a huge arena had been set up for the Exhibition Combat
- The fight was extremely one-sided. The adventurers were demolished.
- When [[Theros]] was down, Vykardan tried to finish him off with a Fireball
- [[Theros]] was saved by a heroic leap from [[Dristuul]]
