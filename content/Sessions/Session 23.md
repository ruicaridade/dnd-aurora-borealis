# Session 23 - Dragon Battle on the Ship

- Combat with 4 Young Dragons on the ship deck
- [[Kallum]] periodically flew over the ship setting it ablaze
- The crew helped put out fires and provided access to a powerful Harpoon Launcher
- [[Kailas]] raged and threw everything 10ft backward — including [[Jaruul]] (who stood his ground) and the Young Black Dragon (knocked clean off the ship)
- [[Theros]] put the Young White Dragon to sleep — it plummeted to its doom
- [[Korin]] used Command: Grovel on the Young Green Dragon — it plummeted too
- [[Dristuul]] harpooned the Young Blue Dragon twice, destroying its face and covering [[Kailas]] in gore
- The adult Blue Dragon fighting [[Kallum]] was killed and eaten by [[Kallum]]
- [[Kallum]] returned to the burning city of [[Rexxentrum]]
- Decision: nothing more could be done for [[Rexxentrum]]. Set course for [[Nicodranas]].
