# Session 24 - Arrival in Nicodranas

- Arrived late evening in [[Nicodranas]]
- Encountered bullies in black and red robes tormenting an old man
- Helped the old man and met [[Nyxara]], who asked to join the party
- On the way to a tavern, a preacher was on the street. [[Dristuul]] used telepathy to play tricks, but something else invaded his mind, causing pain.
- Arranged drinks and rooms at a tavern
- Next day: briefed [[Nyxara]] about their journey so far
- Visited the temple of the Moonweaver; [[Nyxara]] knew someone there
- At sundown, [[Korin]] and [[Jaruul]] approached two figures in black and red robes. [[Dristuul]] established a telepathic link.
- Followed them through the city; they entered a house
- Inside: empty room with a hatch leading underground
- [[Korin]] and [[Jaruul]] confronted a priest speaking of the "Many-Eyed Redeemer"
- [[Theros]] cast Sleep on the priest. Guards noticed — fight started.
- [[Kailas]] raged and killed two guards
- [[Dristuul]] tied up the priest; the priest disintegrated into dust
- [[Kailas]] found a big hole with whispers. [[Theros]] jumped in recklessly, landing on a creature with tentacles and eyes.
- [[Korin]] summoned his horse and jumped into the hole, accidentally crushing [[Theros]]
- The Beholder emerged with [[Theros]] pinned beneath the horse
