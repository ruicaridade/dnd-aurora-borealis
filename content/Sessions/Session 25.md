# Session 25 - The Beholder

- [[Theros]] attacked the Beholder from on top of it
- Everyone scrambled to fight; [[Kailas]] raged and turned invisible briefly
- The Beholder retreated to the hole, hitting everyone with its eye rays
- [[Dristuul]] threw daggers and kept his distance
- The Beholder's ray put [[Theros]] to sleep
- [[Nyxara]] distributed temporary HP to everyone
- Some magical attacks stopped working near the Beholder (anti-magic cone)
- [[Kailas]] barely survived being hit by Disintegration Ray AND Death Ray simultaneously
- [[Dristuul]] ended the fight with a knife through the Beholder's side
- Aftermath: the Beholder's skin started changing color
- [[Jaruul]] ripped off a tentacle (preserved in whisky and flash-frozen)
- [[Theros]] gouged out an eye and jumped into the hole below
- Below: piles of deformed tentacle creatures, alchemy tables, writing on walls
- [[Dristuul]] found a floating black stone — touching it gave visions of the past: the place full of living creatures, the Beholder commanding them, and an enormous monster on a throne with thousands of creatures chanting
- [[Dristuul]] was thrown back when he tried to understand the chanting
