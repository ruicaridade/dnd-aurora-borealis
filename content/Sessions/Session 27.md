# Session 27 - The Ferryman's Journey

- [[The Ferryman]] took them along a river that flowed upward toward the ceiling. 100ft wooden boat.
- [[Dristuul]] tried to speak to [[The Ferryman]] — silence. Then his back tattoo came to life: the donkey gained wings and flew while the dragon destroyed everything.
- [[Korin]] saw a lifeless city where an ancient lightning elemental bound him in chains while whispers blamed him
- [[Kailas]] relived his brother's death in an endless, bloodier loop
- [[Jaruul]] envisioned himself as a mechanical beast over a city before demons destroyed it
- [[The Ferryman]] was showing consequences of the memories they'd given him
- [[Theros]] tried to read [[The Ferryman]]'s mind — found only emptiness. A misplaced Construct from legend.
- Rested in [[Nyxara]]'s hut. [[Jaruul]] and [[Kailas]] kept watch. Shadowy shapes approached. [[Kailas]] collapsed asleep. [[Jaruul]] dragged him inside.
- After 8 hours: [[Korin]] investigated the river — his reflection attacked him. Shadowy figures and tentacles. One vanished underwater; the rest were defeated.
- Light ahead revealed a familiar forest. [[The Ferryman]] was now a regular human.
- Arrived in [[Ixana]], headed to the mansion. [[Sly]] angrily scolded them for taking too long.
- Inside: [[Moelwyn]] lay motionless in arcane hibernation, left arm missing. [[Ishanah]] prayed beside him.
- [[Ishanah]] explained the rings transform wearers into dragons via [[Tiamat]]'s power at the cost of their souls
- The only way to help [[Moelwyn]]: find the Tower of Memory hidden within his mind
