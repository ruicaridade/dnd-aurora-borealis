# Session 30 - Morning at the Mansion

## Morning at the Mansion

- [[Moelwyn]] (who had been asleep for two to three weeks) and [[Ishanah]] attempted to cook breakfast in the kitchen. Results questionable.
- [[Korin]], [[Dristuul]], and [[Theros]] joined them. [[Korin]] asked [[Theros]] to cook but promptly sat down next to [[Moelwyn]] instead, choosing conversation over culinary duties.
- [[Dristuul]] found beef jerky in his pocket, sniffed it, put it back. The group discussed meat and convinced him to share — but everyone politely declined.
- [[Nyxara]] shared her story: she hails from Dal Quor (the Region of Dreams) and is haunted by Vathiriss, a powerful quori. [[Dristuul]] recognized Dal Quor; [[Korin]] had heard of it. [[Moelwyn]] reassured her, saying he might be able to help once he recovers.
- Confirmed the Cobalt Soul has fallen.
- [[Moelwyn]] directed them to his shop in [[Palebank Village]] for helpful items (enchanted goggles, [[Crystal Ball]]).

## Farewell to Sly

- [[Korin]] searched the mansion for [[Sly]] but couldn't find him.
- [[Dristuul]] tracked his scent to the second floor; [[Jaruul]] checked for invisibility — nothing.
- Found [[Sly]] relaxing in a hidden pool.
- [[Dristuul]] gave him some jerky — hastily accepted. The party said their goodbyes.

## Travel to Jinsan

- The party secured a carriage. [[Korin]] took the reins.
- In the back, [[Kailas]], [[Dristuul]] (dealer), and [[Jaruul]] played blackjack:
  - [[Jaruul]] won the first round
  - [[Jaruul]] and [[Kailas]] won the second
  - [[Jaruul]] and [[Theros]] won the third
  - [[Dristuul]] (the dealer) lost a total of 60 gold

## Joey Lokbrass and the Breaker of Chains

- [[Nyxara]] spotted a wagon on the road — [[Joey Lokbrass]]'s travelling shop, Breaker of Chains.
- Items for sale: Rope of Climbing (talks and barks), enchanted mirror, Rings of Resistance, Lucky Stone.
- The enchanted [[Mirror of Desire]] showed each person their deepest longing:
  - [[Theros]]: [[Neerah]]
  - [[Dristuul]]: [[Aldur]] (his brother)
  - [[Kailas]]: his brother
  - [[Nyxara]]: a shadow creature resembling a quori
  - [[Jaruul]]: nothing
  - [[Korin]]: a red-haired elven woman with green eyes (resembling [[Ariameia]])
- The mirror cracked when [[Jaruul]] looked into it — his gaze specifically breaks it. Repaired with Mending by [[Nyxara]], [[Theros]], and [[Jaruul]], with [[Dristuul]] joining in support. Looking again after repair showed the same visions.
- [[Dristuul]] tried to trade his [[Bottomless Flask]] — [[Joey Lokbrass|Joey]] offered two Rings of Resistance in exchange.
- [[Jaruul]]'s chaos: used Disguise Self to look like [[Joey Lokbrass|Joey]], snuck over to Joey's two brothers, and accused the real Joey of being an imposter. The brothers were thrown into utter confusion. [[Jaruul]] then summoned a 10ft illusory giant from the [[Deck of Illusions]]. Joey, terrified, begged the party for help. They "defeated" the illusion and negotiated a massive discount.
- [[Dristuul]] finally obtained his Rope of Climbing.
- The party realised the mirror is a [[Mirror of Desire]] and discussed what the visions might mean.
