# Session 31 - The Shard of Orcus

- Camped as night fell, 1.5 days from the coast. [[Kailas]] noticed necromancy magic emanating from [[Jaruul]].
- The necromancy was coming from the rock [[Jaruul]] took from the Beholder's lair in [[Nicodranas]]
- [[Theros]] identified it as a piece of the Wand of [[Orcus]]. It affects the weak-willed.
- Found a broken wagon on the road with a man and woman hiding something
- The woman stabbed [[Jaruul]]; both shapeshifted into Bodaks, and the mule transformed into a Shoosuva — servants of [[Orcus]] looking for the stone
- Found the corpses of the real people hidden in the wagon
- Camped again and continued toward [[Jinsan]]
