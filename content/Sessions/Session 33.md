# Session 33 - Voyage North and the Ruined Village

- Second day of the voyage aboard [[The Golden Minnow]]; [[Dalaiah]] missing from the upper deck
- Temperature dropped rapidly as they sailed north. [[Kailas]] carved a doorframe (caught by crew).
- [[Korin]] asked Vince about the Briarwoods — only knew of an old case from Whitestone
- [[Dalaiah]] emerged irritated by [[Kailas]]'s carving noise. [[Dristuul]] hid in the crow's nest.
- Arrived at [[Palebank Village]] by nightfall. Captain gave them 24 hours.
- [[Palebank Village]] was in ruins: frozen graveyard, shattered homes, dense fog
- [[Theros]] cast Invisibility on the group to approach
- Reached [[Moelwyn]]'s shop "Moelwyn's Marvelous Mysteries" — oddly untouched, protected by magic
- Inside: two corpses, empty shelves, candles flickering. A massive red-iron maul ([[Pyremaul]]) sat in a glass case.
- Found behind an illusory door: healing potions, coins, gems, [[Crystal Ball]], magic materials
- [[Dristuul]] disarmed a trap and recovered [[Whispersting]] (magical needle)
- [[Kailas]] and [[Korin]] broke the glass case for [[Pyremaul]]; [[Kailas]] kept it
- Outside: cold deepened. Two Frostmourns emerged from the fog.
