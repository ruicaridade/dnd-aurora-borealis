# Session 39 - The Raven Queen's Trial

- **[[Dristuul]]'s ritual**: Attempted to commune with the [[Raven Queen]]. [[Korin]] cast Bane. [[Dristuul]] performed seppuku to start the trial.
- In the dreamscape: a shadowy city, rooftops, a hall of mirrors (all reflections were smiling). A door at the end with footsteps behind him.
- A decayed marketplace with bell traps — [[Dristuul]] moved through acrobatically
- A whisper: "They speak not with words, but with expectations."
- Pulled from a pool. A porcelain mask appeared, then the [[Raven Queen]] herself. She urged him to cherish life and not join her too soon.
- **Resurrection**: The party scrambled to bring [[Dristuul]] back.
  - [[Korin]]: "Come alive."
  - [[Kailas]]: "You better come back."
  - [[Jaruul]]: Cast Aid
  - [[Theros]]: Created illusionary [[Sly]]s
  - [[Nyxara]]: Cast Beacon of Hope
- [[Dristuul]] gasped back to life.
- **Celebration banquet**: [[Moelwyn]] fetched food and returned the money. Elven musicians, dancers, a halfling juggler, exotic food. [[Dristuul]] sang a haunting duet. Battle Dwarfs tournament.
