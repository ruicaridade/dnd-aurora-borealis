# Session 40 - The Old Lady in the Fog

- Party departed around noon after recovering from drinking. [[Moelwyn]] was found sleeping under a table.
- Packed food for a 7-9 day journey. [[Theros]] summoned a unicorn to pull the wagon; [[Korin]] drove.
- Day 1: [[Nyxara]] talked to the unicorn. [[Theros]] noticed something odd about the trees.
- Day 2: Fog rolled in. Patches of moss with drained life and necrotic energy. Eerie silence.
- [[Theros]] and [[Jaruul]] investigated deeper — heard a woman's voice. [[Theros]] got lost.
- Found a corpse with necrotic damage and a torn book page hinting at an ancient corruption source
- [[Theros]] found a hut with an old lady sweeping the porch — she vanished, appeared in the cart, vanished, reappeared inside, then back at the door
- The party burned the hut. "You're going to regret that."
- The fog lifted revealing [[Dristuul]]. A strange mark was found on the wagon where the old lady had sat. [[Kailas]] carved it out.
