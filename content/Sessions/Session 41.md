# Session 41 - The Swamp Village

- The fog thickened. A burning wood smell hung in the air. The trail became muddy.
- Found a swamp village built on stilts
- [[Nyxara]] spotted a shadowy figure. Glass breaking. Lizard creatures were smashing windows and dragging bodies.
- [[Kailas]] raged with thorny vine armor and killed two lizardfolk
- [[Jaruul]] disguised himself as a lizardfolk to blend in
- A Hydra rose from the swamp — wounded, scales torn, clearly sickened
- A glowing stone was embedded in the Hydra's chest
- Behind the Hydra: villagers bound to wooden stakes
