# Session 42 - The Corrupted Hydra

- The Hydra was sickened and rotten — affected by an outside force through the weird object embedded in its chest
- The Hydra relentlessly attacked [[Kailas]]. [[Theros]] stealthily sang. [[Jaruul]] jumped onto the Hydra.
- [[Theros]] crafted an illusion: collars on the three middle heads restraining them
- [[Jaruul]] heard things and lost a turn. A lizardfolk horde was approaching.
- [[Jaruul]] touched the stone in the Hydra's chest — vision: bone throne, endless darkness, horned shadow: "Come closer. Let's be together." He dislodged the stone partially — one head went limp.
- [[Korin]] used Command: Halt. [[Dristuul]] kept his hit-and-hide strategy.
- The Hydra grew two heads from the fallen one. [[Theros]] used Witch Bolt — another head fell.
- [[Jaruul]] cast Thunderwave inside the wound and grabbed the stone. The Hydra reeled.
- [[Kailas]] used Burning Hands for fire damage — cauterizing effect prevented head regrowth
- [[Jaruul]] burrowed INSIDE the Hydra through the wound for another Thunderwave. Another head down.
- [[Kailas]]: "This wasn't a fight, this was a warning for whoever comes next!" — slew the beast. [[Jaruul]] emerged covered in guts.
