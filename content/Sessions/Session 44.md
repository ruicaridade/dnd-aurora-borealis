# Session 44 - Arrival in Shalarag

- [[Korin]] found scales after the fight. [[Theros]] gave the Ioun stone to [[Dristuul]].
- [[Kailas]] returned the fire ring to [[Nyxara]]. Short rest.
- The path sloped upward. From a hilltop: saw [[Shalarag]] — a huge city with a harbour, slums, inner walls, and a citadel. Vineyards and farmland nearby.
- Descended through farms. Main street to inner [[Shalarag]] was about 1km. Carts queued at the wall.
- A kid sold them a genuine map for 10 silver and described the city layout
- Entered freely (only carriages inspected). Found Kings Plaza with a closed church. Booked rooms at the Golden Bough Inn for the night.
- Next morning: learned Emperor [[Kaelor IV Cheon]] was not receiving visitors (3-4 months). Prince [[Malrik]] was missing.
- Split up: [[Dristuul]], [[Theros]], [[Jaruul]], and [[Kailas]] went to the Rusted Coin Inn; [[Korin]] and [[Nyxara]] went to find a smith.
- At the Rusted Coin, [[Dristuul]] spotted a safe haven symbol. Met [[Mara Veyra]] — she recognized him as [[Aldur]]'s brother.
