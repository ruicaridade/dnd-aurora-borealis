# Session 49 - Cleansing the Church

- [[Korin]] healed [[Theros]]. [[Nyxara]] healed [[Kailas]].
- [[High Priest Dorn Velmor]] was freed from [[Falazure]]'s influence. He said the prince's body still lives but his soul is injured — he needs the citadel.
- Decided on a cleansing ritual first. Needed holy water.
- [[Dristuul]] produced holy water from "one of his orifices"
- Prince [[Malrik]]: two wrist cuts (one healed), bruises. Still connected to [[Falazure]]. The cultists had been using royal blood to try to resurrect [[Falazure]] — planning to use the prince's body as a vessel.
- [[Yllira Quen]] sold 120 litres of holy water for 1000 gold
- Cleansed the church with holy water guided by [[Korin]]'s Divine Sense
- During the ritual: shadowy sprites emerged from the prince's chest, the temperature dropped sharply
- [[Korin]] saw the prince's soul anchored below. The [[Chalice of Souls|chalice]] vibrated, revealed a tattoo on the prince. Part of his soul returned.
- A blinding light appeared, then an ethereal platinum sword above the prince's body
- [[Dristuul]] communed with the [[Raven Queen]]. The ritual succeeded. [[Malrik]] regained consciousness.
