# Session 55 - The Fall of Shalarag

- [[Nyxara]] attempted to resurrect [[Aldur]]. [[Korin]] tried channeling the Stormlord — failed. [[Kailas]] tried cauterizing — wounds too old.
- [[Jaruul]] connected to a wisp of [[Aldur]]'s spirit and convinced it to return. [[Dristuul]] spoke in thieves' cant and heard raven wings.
- [[Nyxara]] pulled [[Aldur]]'s soul back — a raven feather fell on his chest, the diamond broke. [[Aldur]] lives!
- Found a well-dressed prisoner. Upstairs: Ned and the guards had been massacred. Someone was attacking the city.
- The gate was demolished. [[Korin]] and [[Kailas]] saved a kid. At the church, [[High Priest Dorn Velmor|Dorn]] recognized the body [[Korin]] carried — the king. Dead for weeks.
- Fled south. The attacking soldiers belonged to the Antamian Empire. Killing everyone — civilians and military alike.
- [[Serena Voss]] held off soldiers and was killed in action
- [[Ishanah]] contacted them telepathically: the Antamian Empire is searching for someone. Head to [[Jurin]].
- Found a wagon, [[Korin]] summoned Loki, and the party escaped [[Shalarag]]
- Dozens of enemy ships firing on the city. This is a slaughter. [[High Priest Dorn Velmor|Dorn]]: the two empires tolerated each other, this war makes no sense.
