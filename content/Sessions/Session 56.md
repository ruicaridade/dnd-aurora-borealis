# Session 56 — The Burial and the Road Home

## The King's Burial

- The party debated going through a mine or around it. [[Theros]] tossed his lucky coin — it pointed them to the caves.
- [[Korin]] asked [[High Priest Dorn Velmor|Dorn]] what would bring the Dynasty to attack [[Shalarag]]. Dorn believed greed was a factor and that they may have somehow learned about what happened with the royal family, though he wasn't certain.
- [[Korin]] asked Dorn what to do with the king's body. They stopped the carriage at a scenic spot and decided to bury [[Kaelor IV Cheon]] atop a hill overlooking [[Shalarag]].
- The party dug the grave. [[High Priest Dorn Velmor|Dorn]] spoke words over the king. [[Korin]] fashioned a cross and planted it beside the grave.
- [[Dristuul]] offered his flask to Dorn — it tasted horrible.

## Camp in the Lava Caves

- The party entered the caves and found a clearing with lava. They set up camp.
- [[Dristuul]] set an alarm trap. Watch order: [[Korin]] → [[Theros]] → [[Kailas]] → [[Dristuul]] → [[Nyxara]] → [[High Priest Dorn Velmor|Dorn]]. They decided to sleep ten hours.

### Korin's Watch
- Uneventful.

### Theros's Watch
- [[Neerah]] woke up and sat next to [[Theros]].
- She was reminiscing about her situation, feeling useless and frustrated, not knowing what to do now that she has lost her Warlock powers.
- [[Theros]] revealed the truth: he **sold his soul to [[Titania]]** in exchange for the power to rescue her. He told her that his father is a thrall to [[Titania]], and that he himself is expected to become her next thrall.
- [[Theros]] also confided that he has given up on singing, that he has been through difficult times and has changed from the person he used to be.
- [[Neerah]], moved by his honesty, regained her spark. She cheered up, looking forward to discovering her new talent or power.

### Kailas's Watch
- [[Kailas]] found himself remembering the empire, and how his mother had always cautioned him about it. He never understood her warnings until the event that changed everything. Her words now ring in his mind.

### Dristuul's Watch — The Black Senate
- [[Aldur]] approached [[Dristuul]] and they talked about coming back from the dead, bonding over the shared experience. They reminisced about family and old memories.
- [[Aldur]] switched to **Draconic** so they wouldn't be overheard, and confided that he is a member of the **[[Black Senate]]** — a secretive organization. He got his powers through them. He described what he had done as part of the group.
- They discussed a woman who wears a **porcelain mask** — [[Aldur]] fancies her. He travels a lot for the organization, has only been a member for a couple of years, and is not high-ranking. [[Dristuul]] noted that only high-ranking members of the [[Black Senate]] wear masks.
- [[Aldur]] offered to introduce [[Dristuul]] to **Lesha**. [[Dristuul]] then started describing someone and mentioned **Robin**.
- [[Dristuul]] revealed that he had **also worked for the [[Black Senate]]** but left. He dodged detailed questions. He warned [[Aldur]] to be careful with the Senate and especially around Robin — not to trust him.
- [[Dristuul]] asked about **Silk** — [[Aldur]] smirked and questioned him further about that relationship. [[Dristuul]] also asked about **Whisper**, but [[Aldur]] didn't remember them. [[Aldur]] mentioned two other members and suggested a meet-up.
- [[Dristuul]] talked about having found a new family in the party, though he admitted he still doesn't think they're "that great."
- The conversation turned to the [[Raven Queen]]. [[Aldur]] was impressed that [[Dristuul]] can speak with her, calling him special. They talked about her more — [[Aldur]] said he likes the Raven Queen based on [[Dristuul]]'s description.
- They said goodnight. [[Dristuul]] hugged [[Aldur]] and told him "I love you." [[Aldur]] said it back.
- [[Mara Veyra|Mara]] said hi.

### Nyxara's Watch
- [[Nyxara]] felt unsettled. A bubble of lava popped and startled her. The rest of the shift was uneventful.

## The Road to Jurin

- Everyone woke up stronger and refreshed.
- After walking for a while, [[Dristuul]] spotted the light at the cave exit. [[Korin]] started singing and [[Neerah]] and [[Aldur]] joined in.
- Outside the cave, the party found the old wagon. They estimated roughly one week to reach the mansion.
- [[Korin]] got confused by the map. [[Nyxara]] helped him get back on track. They passed the Hydra village and continued.
- [[Dristuul]] chatted with [[Aldur]] in Draconic, telling stories. [[Theros]] cast **Comprehend Languages** to eavesdrop. [[Dristuul]] noticed [[Theros]] casting something but couldn't figure out what. [[Theros]] overheard their conversation about the [[Black Senate]].
- [[Theros]] asked party members to write their names into his book: [[Korin]], [[Jaruul]], [[Neerah]], and [[Dristuul]]. Four names now fill the book. He used it to send a message to [[Korin]] (realising [[Ishanah]] had done the same). He then contacted [[Dristuul]] and told him he knows what he and [[Aldur]] were discussing.
- [[Aldur]] told [[Dristuul]] he doesn't want to talk about [[Mara Veyra|Mara]] and asked him not to bring it up.

## Arrival at Jurin — 5th of Unndilar

- The party arrived at [[Jurin]] on the 5th of Unndilar.
- [[Korin]] expressed his happiness to be back at the mansion.
- [[Aldur]] was amazed that the party owns a mansion, not just a house, and was looking forward to seeing it.
- [[Dristuul]] mentioned [[Sly]]. [[Aldur]] was advised not to hurt him.
