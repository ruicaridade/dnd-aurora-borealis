# Session 57 - Return to the Mansion

## Back at the Mansion

- The party returned to their mansion in [[Jurin]] and reunited with [[Sly]].
- [[Prince Malrik Cheon|Malrik]] woke up in the carriage, and [[Korin]] helped him out while [[High Priest Dorn Velmor|Dorn]] panicked over the prince's condition.
- [[Dristuul]] introduced [[Aldur]] to [[Sly]], who reacted with immediate jealousy until Dristuul calmed both of them down.
- Inside, the party found [[Ishanah]], [[Moelwyn]], and [[Brennan]] waiting by the fire.
- [[Ishanah]] stepped away to prepare food while the group gathered to recount everything that happened in [[Shalarag]].

## Wounded, Resting, and Debriefing

- [[Korin]] suggested that the injured recover in the mansion's special healing area.
- [[Dristuul]] brought [[Aldur]] and [[Neerah]] to the healing pool, which Aldur found extremely exciting.
- [[Aldur]] fetched drinks from the cellar and later mentioned overhearing part of a conversation between [[Brennan]] and [[Moelwyn]].
- The group discussed the fall of [[Shalarag]], the prison beneath the city, and the fact that [[Aldur]] had been close to death before the party brought him back.
- They also shared that prisoners in the [[Obsidian Prison]] had been drained of blood, including both [[Aldur]] and [[Neerah]].

## Questions About the Prison and Kallum

- The party learned that [[Laudna Briarwood]] had visited the prison often.
- Based on what Aldur overheard, the king may still have been alive while Aldur and Neerah were imprisoned, raising the possibility that a puppet or stand-in was involved.
- The group concluded that [[Kallum]] and [[Laudna Briarwood]] are likely working together.
- [[Brennan]] spoke about time spent in [[Avernus]] and the danger of Kallum seeking another route to [[Tiamat]]'s power.
- They speculated that the [[Dragon Mask of the Queen]] might be Kallum's next target.

## Splitting Up in Town

- [[Ishanah]] took [[Neerah]] and [[Nyxara]] out to relax, leading them to a massage parlor with full service and three tables.
- Meanwhile, the others headed to [[The Questionable Content]].
- The tavern was almost empty despite it being late afternoon, and the group ordered drinks and snacks from a waiter named Rudy.
- [[Dristuul]] showed Rudy some fish jerky and asked whether he could get some delivered from Urgon at [[Jinsan#The Lazy Crew Inn|The Lazy Crew]] in [[Jinsan]].
- The group drank, talked, and discussed why [[Theros]] does not drink.

## Imperial Soldiers in Town

- After their massages, the women heard heavy boots and saw soldiers in red and gold marching through town.
- One of the soldiers announced, "By order of the crown we seek a fugitive," while showing a sketch.
- [[Nyxara]] recognized the man in the sketch as [[Kailas]] and immediately warned [[Ishanah]] through their telepathic link.
- [[Ishanah]] passed the warning to [[Korin]] and [[Moelwyn]].
- [[Moelwyn]] altered [[Kailas]]'s appearance, and [[Theros]] cast invisibility on him so the group could slip back to the mansion unnoticed.

## Kailas's Secret

- Back at the mansion, the party pressed [[Kailas]] for answers about why the Empire was searching for him.
- Kailas reluctantly revealed that he is tied to the imperial line.
- The party learned that the Emperor is his father and that the Empire wants him dead as a loose strand in the royal family.
- The group decided the safest place to hide him was the pool room.

## Cliffhanger

- A loud knock echoed through the mansion.
- A voice at the door demanded entry by imperial command.
- As the tension peaked, [[Theros]] answered with: "No hablo engleis."
