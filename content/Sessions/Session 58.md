# Session 58 - The Emperor's Bastard

## Imperial Search at the Mansion

- On the 5th of Unndilar at around 8 PM, the Imperial Guards continued pounding on the mansion door in [[Jurin]].
- [[Korin]] stepped outside first and confidently posed as the house guard.
- [[Jaruul]] joined him as the invented noble **Lord Thomas Mhuhrdhehr**, while [[Theros]] disguised himself as an elderly Spanish woman.
- Once the guards entered, [[Dristuul]] played host by pouring whisky and keeping them talking while they explained their purpose.
- The guards split into pairs and began searching the house floor by floor.

## Misdirection and Narrow Escapes

- In the kitchen, [[Dristuul]] casually opened the hidden hatch and led a pair of guards downstairs.
- Upstairs, one guard became suspiciously interested in a bookshelf near the hidden entrance to the pool room.
- [[Theros]] kept up a barrage of loud, confusing Spanish insults and rambling to distract them.
- [[Jaruul]] used **Minor Illusion** to create a noise in another room, pulling the suspicious guard away.
- [[Theros]] subtly disguised the hidden button that opened the way to the pool room.
- One guard noticed humidity on the wall near the hidden chamber, but the suspicion never developed into a discovery.
- In the end, the Imperial Guards found nothing and left the mansion.

## Breaking Kailas Open

- With the immediate danger gone, the party turned on [[Kailas]] and decided to force the truth out of him.
- [[Theros]] disguised himself as a guard and opened the pool-room door, ordering Kailas to come out.
- Kailas reacted with explosive rage, and eerie ethereal music began to play as the tension rose.
- He lunged wildly and tried to strike the nearest target in the groin with his hammer, but [[Theros]] escaped with **Misty Step**.
- [[Korin]] cast **Zone of Truth**, and [[Theros]] followed with **Detect Thoughts**, though he still heard nothing useful.
- Under pressure, Kailas began blurting out embarrassing truths, petty confessions, and personal opinions - including that he likes [[Sly]] the most.

## The Truth of the Throne

- The real revelation came when Kailas admitted that his mother was hated because she had an affair with Emperor [[Corvin Thorian]].
- The party learned that the current emperor is [[Lucian Thorian]], Corvin's son.
- That means Kailas is not merely a bastard of the old emperor - he may be the rightful heir to the throne.
- The group concluded that Lucian knows this and is trying to eliminate him before that claim can matter.

## Planning the Next Move

- The party debated how the Empire might be tracking Kailas.
- [[Moelwyn]] mentioned having a contact in Drar who might be able to provide information.
- [[Brennan]] considered visiting the academy for answers of his own.
- In the end, the group decided to rest for the night and leave at dawn.
- [[Nyxara]] felt deeply uneasy and could not properly settle, convinced that something was wrong.

## Morning Goodbyes

- By morning, the group gathered in the kitchen while [[Korin]] prepared the wagon outside.
- [[Theros]] then shocked everyone by announcing that he was leaving the party to try to live a normal life with [[Neerah]].
- The group shared a toast and said their goodbyes.
- [[Theros]] and [[Neerah]] were given a magical necklace to help them stay hidden.
- The party set out south without them.
- Less than five minutes later, Theros realized he still had the **Bag of Holding** and sprinted after the wagon to return it.

## Trouble on the Road

- As the party traveled south, they passed a family of half-orcs whose children began crying at the sight of [[Korin]].
- [[Dristuul]] angrily called Korin out for racism.
- Soon after, a cart carrying two firbolgs approached.
- They offered polite nods, but something about the encounter immediately felt wrong.
