# Session 59 - Sand Witch Bound

## The Orc on the Road

- Traveling south past sunset on the 6th of Undilar, the party spotted an old orc trudging in the opposite direction under the weight of many bags.
- [[Korin]] spoke with him and learned his name was [[Lory]].
- Lory had fled [[Dumglod]], which he described as ruined and picked apart by pillagers.
- He refused to join the party, despite their offer, and kept heading the wrong way down the road.
- [[Korin]] gave him 10 gold and told him to go to [[Jurin]] and tell the innkeeper that Korin had sent him.
- [[Dristuul]] also urged him to try the fish jerky once it became available there.
- Lory tried to repay the kindness with two Greater Potions of Healing, but the party refused the potions and accepted food instead.
- Before parting, he warned them that [[Dumglod]] had been completely ravaged.

## Arrival in Nanaze

- The party rotated between driving and sleeping through the night to reach their destination faster, with [[Kailas]] taking the first turn.
- By morning they were only a few hours from [[Nanaze]], and the landscape began to shift as dunes appeared in the distance.
- Once a lively travel hub, [[Nanaze]] now felt strained and swollen with refugees.
- Camps crowded the outer districts, strange smells hung in the air, and both inside and outside the city the streets were packed with displaced people.
- Most shops in the outer district were shuttered out of fear of looting.
- The wealthier quarter had more guards and felt safer, but the whole city still carried visible tension.
- The party pushed on and crossed the border into the desert beyond.

## Jacko and the Sand Witch

- A strange vehicle burst out of the dunes at speed and raced toward them.
- Its driver was [[Jacko]], who recognized the party and welcomed them.
- Jacko explained that border formalities mattered less out here than money did.
- He also said that after the destruction of [[Dumglod]], trade now ran directly between [[Nanaze]] and [[Drar]].
- The party rented a pink desert vehicle called the [[Sand Witch]] for 20 gold.
- Jacko warned them to head straight for [[Drar]] and only stop near rocky formations, or a sandworm might find them.

## Crew Assignments and a Rough Start

- The party assigned roles aboard the Sand Witch:
  - [[Kailas]] as driver
  - [[Korin]] as navigator
  - [[Jaruul]] as mechanic
  - [[Nyxara]] as shooter
  - [[Dristuul]] as support
- The engine roared to life with deafening noise, and the party raised a toast to an uneventful trip.
- [[Dristuul]] helped [[Korin]] navigate, even turning the map around for him so he could read it properly.
- At one point, the navigator became distracted and the driver clipped a rock, jolting everyone hard.
- Later, when [[Korin]] spotted a river and asked for the vehicle to slow down, [[Kailas]] slammed the brakes too hard.
- [[Jaruul]] avoided disaster by using **Misty Step**, while [[Nyxara]] was thrown from her seat.
- [[Dristuul]] grabbed Nyxara mid-flight, slowing her enough that she slammed into the sand in front of the vehicle instead of being killed outright.
- [[Dristuul]] then threw himself into the river to freshen up, but the ground soon began to shake and the party quickly fled the area.

## Night in the Rocks

- As the day came to an end, the party searched for a safe rocky formation where they could camp.
- [[Korin]] spotted one option, but [[Nyxara]] found a better sheltered outcropping and the party made camp there.
- [[Jaruul]] performed maintenance on the [[Sand Witch]] while [[Dristuul]] hovered nearby and was eventually asked to touch the engine.
- [[Dristuul]] also asked [[Korin]] to tell the story of **Lisan al Gaib**.
- The group divided up some of the belongings [[Theros]] had left behind.
- [[Nyxara]] set up **Leomund's Tiny Hut** for the night, and the party rested there.

## Morning Ambush

- On the morning of the 7th of Undilar, as the hut faded, [[Jaruul]], [[Korin]], and [[Nyxara]] noticed footprints outside.
- Before they could fully react, [[Korin]] suddenly felt cold.
- [[Sylva Goldenroar]] struck from hiding and stabbed him.
- The fiendish ruler of [[Dumglod]] cursed Korin, ending the session on a brutal surprise attack.
