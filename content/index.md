# Aurora Borealis

> **Campaign Name:** Aurora Borealis
> **Party Name:** Aurora Borealis
> **DM:** kualz
> **System:** D&D 5e
> **Setting:** Custom world — continent of [[Akothis]], with ties to [[Tal'Dorei]] and [[Wildemount]]
> **Campaign Start:** ~May 2021 (in-game); session logs from May 2024
> **Current In-Game Date:** 7th of Undilar, Year 836

---

## Campaign Summary

The Aurora Borealis party finds itself drawn into a centuries-old struggle surrounding [[Tiamat]], the Dragon Queen, whose soul was shattered during [[The Calamity]] and bound into [[The Five Rings of Tiamat|five powerful rings]] scattered across the world. As factions move to either resurrect or permanently destroy the goddess, the party must contend with corrupted allies, draconic gods, and forces from the outer planes — all while uncovering the true history buried beneath the lands they call home.
